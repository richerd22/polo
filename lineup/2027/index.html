How to read the poster

Each day is a block. The first line is that day's headliner; every line after it is set smaller, and the size is billing, not fee and not set time. Within a line, names run left to right in the order printed. Madonna is billed on Friday's second line with "In the Dance Tent" above her name. Daddy Yankee sits on his own line under Sunday with "In the Desert" and no day, and was reported as a fourth headliner in a "special production." Justice is printed at the very bottom as "The Bunker Debut Of JUSTICE - Blinded By The Lights," also with no day. For what billing size means and who fights over it, see the lineup chapter.
