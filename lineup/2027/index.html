What's new on this poster

Madonna, back in the dance tent

The poster prints "In the Dance Tent" over Madonna's name, the only stage named for any act on the 2027 sheet. Her first Coachella set was in 2006, in the Sahara, Coachella's dance tent, on a Sunday. When she joined Sabrina Carpenter on weekend two in 2026, she told the crowd it had been "20 years ago today" that she played the dance tent and called it "a full circle moment" (Billboard). Now the tent is the billing. The moments chapter has the 2026 night.

The Bunker, round two

In 2026 the last line of the poster read "The Bunker Debut of Radiohead Kid A Mnesia." It was not a set: it was an underground cinema under the polo fields, open to anyone with a wristband (FLOOD, Stereogum). The 2027 poster ends the same way, with "The Bunker Debut Of JUSTICE - Blinded By The Lights" and no day. If it works like 2026, it is a piece you visit, not a one-night show. That is a reading of last year, not an announcement. Justice have played Coachella 5 times before (2007, 2008, 2012, 2017, 2024).

How the headliners got here

Dua Lipa: no earlier Coachella set in the archive. Tame Impala: Outdoor Theatre in 2011, then Outdoor Theatre in 2013, then Coachella Stage in 2015, then Coachella Stage in 2019, now headlining Saturday. SZA: Gobi in 2016, then Coachella Stage in 2018, now headlining Sunday. Daddy Yankee: no earlier Coachella set in the archive.

Which day is the reunion day

Share of each day's acts that have played Coachella before: Friday 18 of 54 (33%), Saturday 22 of 52 (42%), Sunday 29 of 49 (59%). Sunday leans most on returning acts.

How to read the poster

Each day is a block. The first line is that day's headliner; every line after it is set smaller, and the size is billing, not fee and not set time. Within a line, names run left to right in the order printed. Daddy Yankee sits on his own line under Sunday with "In the Desert" and no day. For what billing size means and who fights over it, see the lineup chapter.

Questions

When is Coachella 2027?
Two weekends: April 9-11 and April 16-18, 2027, at the Empire Polo Club in Indio, California.
Who is headlining Coachella 2027?
Dua Lipa headlines Friday, Tame Impala Saturday and SZA Sunday. Daddy Yankee is billed "In the Desert" with no day; Yahoo called him a fourth headliner in a "special production."
Is the Coachella 2027 lineup the same both weekends?
As printed, yes: each day block on the poster carries both dates (Friday April 9 & 16, Saturday April 10 & 17, Sunday April 11 & 18). Changes between weekends usually only show once the festival is on.
When do Coachella 2027 tickets go on sale?
General passes go on sale Friday, Nov. 13, 2026, at noon PT, per GV Wire. Yahoo reported an American Express presale the day before. The sources gathered here did not list prices.
What day is Madonna playing Coachella 2027?
Friday, both weekends (April 9 and 16), on the second line with "In the Dance Tent" printed over her name.
What is the Bunker at Coachella?
An underground room built under the polo fields for Radiohead's KID A MNESIA cinema in 2026. The 2027 poster ends with "The Bunker Debut Of JUSTICE - Blinded By The Lights"; what that is had not been announced as of Nov. 11, 2026.
Are Coachella 2027 set times out?
Not as of November 11, 2026. Set times and stages usually come out shortly before the festival; this page will add them when they do.
Has Tame Impala played Coachella before?
Yes, in 2011, 2013, 2015, 2019, including a 2019 headline set alongside Childish Gambino and Ariana Grande.
How many Coachella 2027 acts have played Coachella before?
70 of 157, matched against every earlier lineup in the archive (1999-2026, every edition that happened). The rest have no earlier appearance there.
