## A) The 2020 story - chapter outline

### Ch. 1 - The calendar that collapsed
- Coachella 2020 was set for April 10-12 and 17-19. Goldenvoice announced on **March 10, 2020** that it was moving to **Oct 9-11 and 16-18**, "at the direction of" Riverside County and local health authorities. April tickets would be honored. **[verified]** - Michael Juliano, *Time Out LA*, Mar 11 2020: https://www.timeout.com/los-angeles/news/coachella-2020-is-getting-postponed-until-october-due-to-coronavirus-031020
- Billboard broke the October move the day before. **[verified headline]** - *KESQ*, Mar 9 2020: https://kesq.com/news/2020/03/09/billboard-coachella-stagecoach-may-be-moved-to-october/
- The lineup was announced Jan 2 2020, with Rage Against the Machine, Travis Scott and Frank Ocean as headliners. On the original opening day, Apr 10, YouTube released the documentary *Coachella: 20 Years in the Desert*. **[verified]** - *Know Your Meme* event timeline: https://knowyourmeme.com/memes/events/coachella-2020 ; *Deseret News*, Apr 10 2020: https://www.deseret.com/entertainment/2020/4/10/21216853/coachella-valley-music-and-arts-festival-youtube-documentary-20-years-in-the-desert/

### Ch. 4 - Who got hurt
- **Out-of-home industry (OAAA):**
  - Q2 2020 revenue fell **45%** year over year to $1.48B, and the first half fell 25.2%. **[verified]** - John Weller, *Billboard Insider*, Aug 27 2020: https://billboardinsider.com/ooh-advertising-down-45-in-q2-2020/
  - Q3 fell **36%**, and the year to date was down 28.5% ($4.6B). Cinema ad revenue was zero. **[verified]** - *OAAA* release, Nov 19 2020: https://oaaa.org/news/out-of-home-advertising-down-36-in-q3-2020-down-28-5-ytd/
- **Outfront:** full-year 2020 revenue was $1,236.3M, down **30.6%**. Q4 was down 31.2%. **[verified]** - Outfront 8-K earnings release, Feb 25 2021: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1579877/000157987721000031/ex991123120earningsrelease.htm
- **Lamar:** full-year 2020 revenue was $1.57B, down **10.5%** from $1.75B. **[verified]** - *The Advocate* (Baton Rouge), likely Kristen Mosbrucker, early 2021 (undated in text): https://www.theadvocate.com/baton_rouge/news/business/article_927fb41c-785d-11eb-af6a-ef193f5c9050.html
- Lamar fell much less than Outfront. The reason is probably that Lamar leans toward roadside and local inventory while Outfront leans toward transit and big metros **[inference]**.
- **Valley economy:**
  - The Coachella Valley Economic Partnership estimated about **$700M** in losses from the absence of Coachella and Stagecoach. **[verified]** - Olivia Perreault, *TicketNews*, Apr 13 2020: https://ticketnews.com/2020/04/coachella-postponement-local-economy (KESQ repeats the figure in its Jun 10 2020 piece above.)
  - Valley cities were drawing on emergency reserves, citing the Desert Sun. **[verified, secondhand]** - same TicketNews piece.
- **Pre-pandemic baseline for scale:** AdQuick tracked 378 I-10 boards. April prices rose 31% against the rest of the year and 14.2% against 2018. Boards cost $8K-$20K for four weeks and sold out in January. **[verified]** - *Billboard Insider*, Apr 11 2019: https://billboardinsider.com/demand-surges-for-coachella-festival-billboards/ ; *OAAA* blog, Apr 22 2019: https://oaaa.org/blog-posts/billboards-headlining-at-coachella/

### Ch. 5 - 2022: the return
All points in this chapter come from the *Billboard Pro* piece above (April 2022 per inference) **[verified]**:
- DASH TWO had **15 boards booked**, down from 20 in 2020. Sesto: "It was just difficult this year"; "There's a lot of skepticism still"; few huge advertisers.
- Lamar sold **66 spots, against 64 in 2019**. Lamar's Ian Dallimore: "We're seeing a lot of this business come back bigger, because people feel more comfortable."
- AdQuick counted **548 boards** on the LA-to-festival route, against 533 in 2019 and 485 in 2018.
- Inventory squeeze: local car dealers and casinos used to sublet their boards to Coachella advertisers at a discount. They **stopped including those boards in contracts after the 2020-21 cancellations**.
- Sesto estimated about 75 boards in the 23 miles from Banning to Palm Springs.
- Prices were $15K-$40K per board (MilkMoney's Sam Keywanfar). MilkMoney had 26 advertisers.
- Named 2022 boards: Louis the Child, Omar Apollo, Travis Scott, Billie Eilish (Adobe, via MilkMoney), Kendall Jenner's 818 tequila, and Olly.

The defining 2022 board:
- Travis Scott / Cactus Jack's "Psst... / Looking for UTOPIA? / Wrong way!" boards stood off I-10, facing away from Indio. He had been removed as a headliner after Astroworld. **[verified]** - Brenton Blanchet, *Complex*, Apr 11 2022: https://www.complex.com/music/travis-scott-utopia-album-promoted-on-billboards-near-coachella-removed-from-headlining

## B) Google Street View as a source

### What exists
- **Historical imagery ("See more dates")** launched on desktop Google Maps on Apr 23 2014. It draws on Street View collections **back to 2007**, and some US highway imagery is older. Coverage depends on the archive and varies by location. **[verified]** - Iain Thomson, *The Register*, Apr 23 2014: https://www.theregister.com/2014/04/23/google_adds_a_sense_of_history_to_street_view_with_archive_footage/
- It came to the Android and iOS apps in May 2022. **[verified headline]** - *TechCrunch*, May 24 2022: https://techcrunch.com/2022/05/24/google-maps-street-view-celebrates-15-years-with-historical-imagery-on-mobile-new-camera-and-more
- Google Earth's official how-to (Pegman, "See more dates", timeline slider) gives no coverage dates. **[verified]** - https://developers.google.com/maps/documentation/earth/historical-street-view

### Does it cover I-10 Beaumont to Indio, and Gene Autry Trail / CA-111?
- **Not verified for these exact roads.** I couldn't check capture dates from here: the official metadata API needs a key, and unofficial date-scraping tools would breach the terms below.
- **[inference]** I-10 is a major interstate, and Gene Autry Trail and CA-111 are major Palm Springs arterials. All three almost certainly have several captures since about 2008-2011.
- **[inference]** Captures come every one to a few years, not every year. Each is labeled by month and year only.
- **Manual check:** drop Pegman at a few points (Cabazon, the Whitewater/CA-111 split, Gene Autry at I-10, Monroe St exit, and others), open "See more dates", and log which month-years exist.

### Can individual festival boards from specific springs be found?
- **[inference]** Sometimes, by luck only. Street View cars don't time captures to festival weeks. A capture dated **April** of a festival year, on the right stretch of road, may show the boards. March or May captures might catch boards going up or still standing.
- **[inference]** Billboards are usually readable in captures taken close to the road. Boards that are far off, angled away, or on the opposite side may be unreadable.
- **Prior work: [not found].** I found no article or Reddit post where anyone had used Street View to recover Coachella boards. General precedent exists for using Street View history as a research record - e.g. gentrification studies (TACity, Mar 24 2021: https://tacity.co.uk/2021/03/24/using-google-street-view-archive-as-gentrification-research/).

### What Google's terms allow
Official page: Google Geo Guidelines, https://about.google/brand-resource-center/products-and-services/geo-guidelines/ . The older path www.google.com/permissions/geoguidelines/ redirects there. **[verified]**

| Use | Allowed? | Source line |
|---|---|---|
| **Link** to a Street View location | Yes - linking with text or buttons is fine | Geo Guidelines, Maps web/apps |
| **Embed** a Street View panorama on a website | Yes, no permission needed. Commercial integration goes through Maps Platform APIs | Geo Guidelines, Street View web/apps |
| **Screenshot** Street View | **No.** "You may not screenshot Street View imagery or remove it from embedded sources for any purpose." | Geo Guidelines, Street View |
| Print | No, in any form | Geo Guidelines, Street View |
| Trace or digitize, analyze with apps, download for offline use, stitch | **No**, including for academic and nonprofit projects | Geo Guidelines, Street View prohibitions |
| Attribution | Always required; never crop or move it to a footer. Automatic in embeds | Geo Guidelines, Attribution |

**Implication for the archive:** no Street View screengrabs of boards, and no redrawn or recreated boards based on Street View. Batch-scanning panoramas with a script also counts as "analyzing imagery with applications," so finding boards has to be manual browsing.

### Official tools for pointing at a specific panorama
- **Maps URLs** - no API key, free. Format: `https://www.google.com/maps/@?api=1&map_action=pano&pano=<PANO_ID>&viewpoint=<lat>,<lng>&heading=<deg>&pitch=<deg>&fov=<deg>`. `viewpoint` alone loads the nearest current panorama, which can change after a new drive. `pano` pins a specific panorama, and `viewpoint` is used only if the pano ID isn't found. **[verified]** - https://developers.google.com/maps/documentation/urls/get-started
- **Maps Embed API, Street View mode** - an iframe at `https://www.google.com/maps/embed/v1/streetview?key=KEY&pano=<PANO_ID>&heading=&pitch=&fov=`. It takes `pano` or `location`. **[verified]** - https://developers.google.com/maps/documentation/embed/embedding-map . Cost: "free to use with unlimited requests", with no QPS or daily limits, but a key is required. **[verified]** - https://developers.google.com/maps/documentation/embed/usage-and-billing
- **Storing pano IDs** - Google lets you store panorama IDs indefinitely. Other content can't be cached. **[verified]** - Street View Static API policies: https://developers.google.com/maps/documentation/streetview/policies
- **Not verified:** whether a pano ID from an *older* capture still renders the old imagery in the Embed API and Maps URLs, rather than snapping to the latest. Pano-ID format notes exist (reanna.neocities.org, Feb 25 2025: https://reanna.neocities.org/blog/street-view-pano-ids) but don't settle it. **Test with one known old panorama before building around it.**
- **Avoid the Street View Static API** (fetching JPEGs) for this. It's paid, it has caching limits, and its output is effectively a rehosted image, so it risks violating the no-removal rule. **[inference]**

### Recommended citation method (within the terms)
1. Find the board by hand in Google Maps with "See more dates".
2. Copy the Share link from that historical view. Also record:
   - pano ID (if shown in the URL)
   - lat/lng, heading, pitch and fov
   - the **capture date exactly as Google labels it** (e.g. "Apr 2019")
   - your access date
3. On the page:
   - **Text citation:** "Google Street View, I-10 westbound near [exit], imagery dated Apr 2019, accessed 2026-11-02" + link.
   - **Optional live view:** the Maps Embed API iframe with `pano=` (free, attribution built in), *only if* the old-pano test passes. Otherwise use a "View on Google Street View" button.
4. Don't screenshot, crop, trace or describe it as "our photo." Keep Street View visually separate from your own or contributed photos, which remain the archive's primary images.
5. Treat Street View as corroboration: a board was at this spot in this month and year. Primary proof still comes from dated press, social posts and photos.
6. Commercial caveat: if the archive ever earns money (ads, sponsorship), the guidelines route commercial use through Maps Platform. The Embed API is already Maps Platform, so the embed route stays valid **[inference]**.
