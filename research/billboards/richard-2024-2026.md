# Other leads: Indio, the valley, the pass (search-only, from the same session)


- Indio's nickname is "The City of Festivals"; motto "The Place to Be." Sources disagree on why: Chamber of Commerce adoption, the Indio International Tamale Festival, or Coachella/Stagecoach plus the Date Festival. A 1970s Chamber slogan was "the Hub of the Valley." [search-only - Wikipedia, blocked]
  - Wikipedia - "Indio, California" - n.d. - https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indio,_California
- 2020 census: 89,137 residents; 2010: 76,036 (about +17%). 2,682.7 people per square mile. [search-only]
  - U.S. Census Bureau - "QuickFacts: Indio city, California" - n.d. - https://www.census.gov/quickfacts/fact/table/indiocitycalifornia
- A Goldenvoice-commissioned study (reported 2013) said the festival put more than $90M into Indio's economy. [search-only]
