## 1. Do LaB

**Who runs it**
- Founded and run by three brothers: Dede, Josh and Jesse Flemming (Josh and Jesse are twins). Jesse books the music, Josh designs the structures, Dede runs production and deals with Goldenvoice. [LAW25]; same split in [NPR22] (Jesse = music, Josh = design/structure, Dede = logistics).
- EDM Identity also names Dream Rockwell as a co-founder of The Do LaB "in 2004." [EDMID24]. Wikipedia's Lightning in a Bottle page lists Dream Rockwell, Josh and Jesse Flemming as hosts of the first LIB. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lightning_in_a_Bottle
- Roots: renegade parties going back to a 1999 birthday party in the mountains, which became an annual event and then a company. [EW23]; "worked together as Do LaB since the late 1990s, starting with renegade parties." [POLL24]. They ran a downtown LA warehouse that police and fire officials shut down, which Josh says forced them to go legal. [NPR22]

**Start year at Coachella - the 2004 / 2005 / 2006 conflict**
- 2004: Variety says Do LaB "started at Coachella in 2004 as a last-minute, unofficial party." [VAR23]. NPR says their first presence was 2004, sneaking in speakers after hours. [NPR22]
- 2005: The FADER (whose 2025 piece is framed as a 20th anniversary) dates the first installation to 2005. [FADER25]. LA Weekly: "an art installation with a few speakers at Coachella 2005." [LAW25]. EW: began as an art installation in a dome in 2005. [EW23]. Pollstar, paraphrasing the brothers: in 2005 a friend who was an art curator at the festival invited them to build on site. [POLL24]
- 2006: EDM Identity calls 2006's "Lucent Dossier Domnation" the first Do LaB *stage* at Coachella. [EDMID24]. The FADER and EW say 2006 is when Paul Tollett overbooked two DJs and put them in the Do LaB dome with extra sound and lights - Dede calls this "the green light" to play music. [FADER25] [EW23]
- **Proposed resolution:** The Do LaB as a company/collective dates to 2004 (the year of the first Lightning in a Bottle). Its first Coachella appearance was 2005, as an invited art installation with unofficial after-hours music. 2006 is the first year Coachella itself put booked music in it - effectively its first sanctioned "stage" year. The "2004" Coachella claims (Variety, NPR) are the outliers and likely conflate the company's founding with its first Coachella; the brothers' own recollections in FADER/Pollstar/EW point to 2005. Inference, not a stated fact.

**Growth timeline (from the brothers' accounts)**
- 2005: Dome with a water feature that "didn't work very well"; crew ended up "showering in it and drinking from it"; wind mangled it on day one. After the main stage shut off, a party started there - "That's the first time we realized, 'This could be more than an installation.'" (Jesse). [FADER25]. Crowd of roughly a thousand after midnight. [EW23]. Tiny budget; they misted people and handed out orange slices in 107-degree heat. [POLL24]
- 2006: Tollett's overbooked DJs placed in the dome. [FADER25]
- 2008: Went to immersive 360-degree environments - "went way bigger than I think they wanted us to" (Dede). [FADER25]
- 2009: Coachella briefly ordered their sign taken down because the festival had no branding then; they talked Tollett into keeping it. [FADER25]
- 2010: "Misting Oasis," moved to the middle of the festival. [EDMID24]. Performance stage added for troupe Lucent Dossier, pushing it toward "more of a stage and less of an art installation." [FADER25]. Original installation also referred to as the "Lucent Misting Oasis," with water raining down from a sign. [LAW25]
- 2011: "Fish fry," inspired by Santiago Calatrava - Josh calls it their "lowest point"; it "barely stood up." [FADER25]
- 2012: High-intensity water cannons in 100-plus-degree heat; dance platforms added. [EDMID24]
- 2014: Moved to a corner of the grounds near the terrace and food court, with a backstage; went from circular to open design. [EDMID24]. Dede: they began calling it "the official sixth stage at Coachella." [FADER25]. Structure "Eepeerts" built from utility poles. [FADER25]
- 2015-2016: "Big Fish," fish-tail shaped, soundboard/tree house in an oak tree. [EDMID24] [FADER25]
- 2017-2018: "Beacon," designed with Heather Shaw of Vita Motus; windstorm damaged the fabric and it was restored before weekend two. [EDMID24]
- 2019-2023: "Warrior One," colorful paneled roof. [EDMID24]. 2022: 200-by-200-foot tent of colorful triangular fabric; crowds up to about 10,000 at night. [NPR22]
- Poster status: within a few years Coachella listed Do LaB as an official installation in tiny type at the bottom of the poster - Dede called it a "badge of honor." [EW23]. Still absent from the official set-time grid; lineups are announced separately, usually late March. [VAR23]
- Grown to about four times its original size. [VAR23]

**How it books independently**
- Jesse books it himself, outside the festival-wide booking Paul Tollett runs. Aim: "catch artists right before they're about to pop"; "if it doesn't make you feel good, it's not for us." [LAW25]
- Dede: "We're not trying to sell tickets." Closing slots are sometimes booked hours before they start. [VAR23]
- Surprise tradition "invented out of necessity" - headliner contracts finalize late, sometimes day-of. Jesse: "I'm pretty sure it started because we left it open to see what would happen." [EDMH23]
- More pop acts since about 2022. [FADER25]

**Signature surprise sets (with years where sourced)**
- 2014: ODESZA on the bill before they broke. [FADER25]
- 2015: Bonobo - "the first big artist that was outside of our sphere of friends"; the response led them to "leave a couple surprises every year" (Jesse). [FADER25]. Bonobo b2b Chet Faker confirmed a couple of hours before the slot (year not given). [EDMH23]
- 2017: Kaskade was the planned surprise but tweeted it; security said the area couldn't hold the crowd, and Skrillex played instead. [FADER25]. Skrillex's 2017 surprise Do LaB set also covered by EARMILK, Apr 24, 2017 - https://earmilk.com/news/20170424/a-look-inside-skrillexs-surprise-do-lab-performance
- 2017: RÜFÜS DU SOL surprise set. Dancing Astronaut, May 2017 - https://dancingastronaut.com/2017/05/stream-rufus-du-sols-surprise-lab-set-coachella/
- 2018: ZHU's Blacklizt surprise set. Dancing Astronaut, Apr 2020 - https://dancingastronaut.com/2020/04/good-morning-mix-revisit-zhus-2018-blacklizt-surprise-set-at-coachella/. FISHER and Chris Lake drew the biggest afternoon crowds Do LaB had seen. [FADER25]
- 2019: Major Lazer with Diplo. EDMTunes, Apr 16, 2019 - https://www.edmtunes.com/2019/04/16/surprise-major-lazer-and-friends-knock-out-the-dolab-with-diplo-during-coachella/
- 2022: Diplo, Rebecca Black, MEUTE (weekend one). [NPR22]. Skream dubstep set. [FADER25]
- 2023: James Blake (hip-hop set on vinyl). [FADER25]. ODESZA surprise set. [EW23]
- 2024: Billie Eilish - Josh estimates "over 20,000 people," the largest Do LaB crowd ever. Anderson .Paak as DJ Pee .Wee. [FADER25]
- 2025: Mau P, Zeds Dead, Bob Moses. Coachella Magazine (no author/date) - https://coachellamagazine.com/do-lab-returns-to-coachella-2026/
- 2026: GRiZ and Sub Focus led a surprise-driven Friday. Mixmag Caribbean - https://mixmagcaribbean.com/read/coachella-2026-griz-and-sub-focus-lead-surprise-driven-friday-at-do-lab-stage-news
- Others named without year: Richie Hawtin, LP Giobbi, Madeon, SG Lewis, John Summit. [VAR23]
- **Zedd: no source found** for a Zedd surprise set at Do LaB. See Unconfirmed.

**Water / misters**
- Water has been central since the start; Dede: they focused on water because "we knew how intense it was out here." Crowd members are sprayed with water guns. [LAW25]
- Misters cool the crowd; performers sprayed the audience with flower-decorated water guns (2022). [NPR22] [EW23]
- 2012 water cannons; misters and cannons remain part of daytime cooling. [EDMID24]
- Wristband heat-map data reportedly show Do LaB draws the most consistent crowd of any Coachella stage. [VAR23]

**2026 incident**
- Do LaB closed on Friday night, Apr 10, 2026, after a light reportedly fell on a woman during a surprise John Summit set; later sets including Andy C were cancelled. Details reported by attendees on Reddit/X/TikTok; no official statement at time of writing. High winds also caused Anyma to cancel that night. Patrick Hinton, Mixmag, Apr 12, 2026 - https://mixmag.net/read/coachellas-do-lab-dance-stage-closes-after-falling-light-reportedly-injures-crowd-member-news

**Spin-off: Lightning in a Bottle (LIB)**
- Name came from a friend's private July birthday party, 2001-2003. Wikipedia, "Lightning in a Bottle" - https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lightning_in_a_Bottle
- Start-year conflict: Wikipedia says the first LIB was 2004 at Gold Creek Ranch, Angeles National Forest, then a 2005 hiatus. [Wikipedia LIB]. EW says LIB started in 2006 [EW23]; Daily Trojan's "10 years" piece also gives 2006 (no author/date in fetch) - https://dailytrojan.com/2015/05/19/festival-celebrates-10-years. LA Weekly says LIB has run "even longer than" the Coachella stage. [LAW25]. 2026 press calls LIB "an independent festival for 23 years running." BroadwayWorld, Nov 11, 2025 - https://www.broadwayworld.com/los-angeles/article/Do-LaB-Reveals-Music-Lineup-For-Lightning-In-A-Bottle-2026-20251111
- **Proposed resolution:** 2004 was the first event under the name (a smaller gathering); 2006 at Live Oak Camp is the first three-day public festival, which is why some outlets count from 2006. Inference.
- Locations (per Wikipedia LIB): 2004 Gold Creek Ranch; 2006-2008 Live Oak Camp, Santa Barbara County (about 1,000 at the first one); 2009 hiatus; 2010 Oak Canyon Ranch, Silverado; 2013 Lake Skinner, Winchester; 2014-2016 San Antonio Reservoir Recreation Area, Bradley (also Daily Trojan); 2019 onward Buena Vista Aquatic Recreation Area, Kern County (move approved unanimously by Kern supervisors - Bakersfield Californian, Feb 26, 2019, cited by Wikipedia). 2017-2018 site not named in the fetch.
- 2020 cancelled (COVID; KGET, Mar 13, 2020). 2021 cancelled (Billboard, Mar 1, 2021). Both via Wikipedia LIB. NPR notes some 2020 ticket holders felt shortchanged by partial refunds. [NPR22]
- 2022 restart at Buena Vista. 2023 had a throwback-rave feel but under-sold; 2024 the talent budget went up (Skrillex, Skream, Labrinth, M.I.A., Fatboy Slim, Tipper...). [POLL24]. Jesse cited competition from Live Nation/AEG and radius clauses. [POLL24]
- 2025: about 26,000 attendees; a Kern Economic Development Corporation report credited it with $14M for the county economy (BakersfieldNow, Sep 5, 2025, via Wikipedia LIB).
- 2026: May 20-24, Buena Vista Lake; Empire of the Sun, Mau P, Sara Landry, Zeds Dead, Chase & Status, Mochakk, Barry Can't Swim, Tinashe. [BroadwayWorld, Nov 11, 2025]. Wikipedia's "30,000 in 2026" figure is unverified.
- The brothers start building LIB about five days after Coachella weekend two. [POLL24]
- **Other Do LaB productions:** none confirmed in the sources fetched beyond Do LaB @ Coachella and LIB.

## 2. Quasar

- Announced on social media the week before Mar 20, 2024; a Goldenvoice-presented dance stage for three-hour-plus DJ sets, both weekends. Jack Ramage, DJ Mag, Mar 20, 2024 - https://djmag.com/news/coachella-unveils-new-dance-music-stage-quasar
- Location: next to the Eldorado Polo Club (DJ Mag); Mixmag Asia says it sits where the old Sahara tent stood. Amira Waworuntu, Mixmag Asia, Mar 21, 2024 - https://mixmag.asia/read/coachella-new-quasar-stage-extended-dj-sets-michael-bibi-rufus-du-sol-news. The 2024 Sahara was moved south to sit beside Quasar. Appeal-Democrat/pressreader, Apr 18, 2024 - https://www.pressreader.com/usa/marysville-appeal-democrat/20240418/281883008388583
- Physical: two "monolithic" LED walls, asymmetrical, "really architectural and simple"; walls show a live Unreal Engine version of the Coachella landscape driven in real time. Designed by Vita Motus (Heather Shaw, who also helped design Do LaB and has attended every Coachella since 1999). Shaw heard about it at the end of January 2024 - under four months to build, her tightest timeline. Sydney Bucksbaum, EW (via Yahoo), Apr 12, 2024 - https://ca.news.yahoo.com/coachellas-newest-most-ambitious-stage-174230789.html
- Why it exists: Shaw links the push to 2023's replacement headliners (Fred again.., Skrillex, Four Tet after Frank Ocean dropped out); 60-90 minute sets are too short for a full arc. [EW 2024, same link]
- Curation: Goldenvoice books it (EW 2024). Format is one long set per night, often b2b/b3b.
- Note on "all-night/sunrise": sources describe *extended* (3-4 hour) sets and a 2024 "sunset" b2b; no source describes sunrise sets. Coachella's curfew makes sunrise implausible - treat "sunrise" as unconfirmed.
- 2024 bookings: W1 Honey Dijon + Green Velvet; Michael Bibi (first show after CNS lymphoma treatment); Jamie xx / Floating Points / Daphni b3b. W2 RÜFÜS DU SOL DJ set; Eric Prydz b2b Anyma sunset set; Diplo + Mau P. [DJ Mag] [Mixmag Asia]
- 2025: Martinez Brothers b2b Loco Dice; Barry Can't Swim b2b Salute b2b 2manydjs; Tiësto; Test Pilot (deadmau5) b2b ZHU / Blacklizt; Idris Elba with Kaskade Redux; Alesso b2b Gorgon City. This Song Is Sick (no date/author in fetch) - https://thissongissick.com/post/coachella-quasar-2025/
- 2026: PAWSA, David Guetta, Fatboy Slim (W1); Armin van Buuren x Adam Beyer, DJ Snake (Pardon My French), Sara Landry (Blood Oath) (W2). One Stop Rave, Feb 8, 2026 - https://onestoprave.com/blogs/one-stop-rave-blog/%F0%9F%8E%A7-coachella-2026-quasar-stage-lineup-reveal-what-you-need-to-know. Flaunt's 2026 recap also names Tiga and Deep Dish on the Friday with PAWSA, so "one artist per day" is not strictly true. https://www.flaunt.com/post/coachella-2026-a-redbull-mirage

## 4. Sahara

- Existed at the first Coachella (Oct 1999) as one of three "rave tents": Mojave, Gobi and Sahara, alongside a main stage and a second stage. [PSL]
- First bill included A-Trak, Juan Atkins, Kevin Saunderson and Richie Hawtin; LA Times calls it "as old as Coachella." Early on it was pushed to the far edge of the grounds, beside what became the Rose Garden VIP area, with only a few light panels. [LAT23]
- No Coachella in 2000; Goldenvoice ran Nocturnal Wonderland at Empire Polo that September instead. [WIKI-C]
- 2006: Daft Punk's pyramid set and a Madonna surprise appearance - fans date the EDM boom to that weekend. [LAT23]
- 2011: Odd Future played one of the tent's first live (non-DJ) shows. [LAT23]
- 2013: enlarged to 80 ft tall [WIKI-C]; hangar-like, 20,000 capacity, built/lit by AG Lighting and Sound (Andrew Gumper). [PLSN13]
- 2018: moved and grown 25%; nearly 100 ft tall, 250-ft-wide stage, rated for 90 mph winds, on permanent concrete footings about two feet below the grass. Built by Koen De Puysseleir and Andrew Gumper (AG, Las Vegas); audio Rat Sound; flames/lasers Pyrotecnico. [LAT23]. Wikipedia says the 2018 move was west toward the Eldorado Polo Club and the stage was turned to a long side for a wider view. [WIKI-C]. Conflict: LA Times says "to the center of the grounds."
- 2018 onward: shift toward rap (Post Malone, Lil Uzi Vert). 2019: Blackpink's U.S. festival debut. 2022: 21 Savage with Metro Boomin; Fred again.. [LAT23]
- Booked by Paul Tollett's team. [LAT23]
- 2024: rebuilt and moved again - new steel frame replacing aluminum, 320 ft long, ~1,500 tons of concrete footings poured from December, south of its old site between Avenue 51 and Polo Road. Jason Brown (Goldenvoice VP artist production): "There is so much more weight capacity." Ema Sasic, Desert Sun (via Yahoo), Feb 6, 2024 - https://www.yahoo.com/entertainment/coachella-2024-sahara-tent-moving-003831144.html; Peter Volpe, EDM Maniac, Feb 6, 2024 - https://edmmaniac.com/?p=3365. A new grass hill and "Sahara Express Walkway" were added; some fans said the walk to the main stage was about 15 minutes. [Appeal-Democrat, Apr 18, 2024]
- Earlier name: no source found that the Sahara was ever called anything else.

## 5. Mojave

- One of the three original 1999 tents. [PSL]
- Named after the Mojave Desert. [WIKI-C]
- 2013: 60x40 ft truss grid and stage; "noodles" (motion-controlled hanging tubes) used as a 3D projection surface. [PLSN13]
- 2017: moved behind an access road; 2018 enlarged and moved to the Sahara's former spot near the Rose Garden. [WIKI-C]
- Indie rock / rising acts; second-largest tent. Discotech guide, Sep 10, 2020 (updated Jan 26, 2022) - https://discotech.me/festivals/guide-to-different-coachella-stages/
- Capacity "12,000 to 15,000" is a social-media claim (X post) - reported by attendees, unverified.

## 6. Gobi

- One of the three original 1999 tents [PSL]; named after the Gobi Desert [WIKI-C].
- 2013: 60x40 ft Versa Stage and roof; built by RKDE, All Access Staging, Screenworks. [PLSN13]
- Moved behind an access road in 2017. [WIKI-C]
- Smallest of the classic tents; emerging acts, shaded, rarely crowded. [Discotech]
- Retirement: no source found that Gobi has been retired; Despacio's 2023 location was described as "to the right of the Sonora and Gobi tents." Groove Cartel (no date/author) - https://thegroovecartel.com/featured/coachella-hidden-gem-despacio/

## 7. Coachella Stage (main stage)

- In use since 1999 [WIKI-C]; Palm Springs Life calls it "main stage" in 1999 [PSL]. Wikipedia's lineup tables use "Main Stage" in early years and "Coachella Stage" later, with no stated rename date - https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coachella_Festival_line-ups
- 2013: built by Brown United, six legs, nicknamed "Optimus Brown" by stagehands; ~165,000 lbs of gear on a 500,000-lb rating; sound by Rat Sound. [PLSN13]

## 8. Outdoor Theatre

- In use since the start, next to the Coachella Stage [WIKI-C]; second-largest stage, known for sunset sets [Discotech].
- 2013: 65x45 ft roof, 60x48 ft stage, Visions Lighting; sliding traveler trusses for fast changeovers. [PLSN13]
- Not in 1999 per Palm Springs Life, which lists "main stage" and "second stage" - the second stage may be its ancestor; Wikipedia's lineup tables show "Outdoor Theatre" in its first table. Unresolved.

## 9. Yuma

- Debuted 2013 - confirmed. "The mysterious new Yuma Tent": indoor-nightclub design with wood floors, disco balls, faux Baroque portraits, couches, air conditioning. Night one: Four Tet (90-minute improvised opener), DJ Harvey, Seth Troxler (delayed), Luciano. Dancing Astronaut Staff, Apr 13, 2013 - https://dancingastronaut.com/2013/04/exploring-indio-day-one-at-coachella-yuma-yuma-yuma/
- Sixth stage of 2013, in the terrace food area near the Heineken Dome; Luciano, Four Tet, Richie Hawtin. [PLSN13]
- Intent: "a sophisticated space that dials down the noise and strobe lights in favor of thoughtful sounds and underground acts." [WIKI-C]
- Lighting designer Steve Lieberman (SJ Lighting) for years; lights only, no video wall, kept dark and minimal; more bays added over time. LSi, Jun 2, 2025 - https://lsionline.co.uk/news/chauvet-creates-coachella-club-atmosphere
- Custom soundproofing mentioned. Harry Levin, MusicTech (2026, exact date not shown) - https://musictech.com/features/interviews/why-festivals-are-booking-hi-fi-sound-systems-on-their-lineups/
- Name: Yuma Desert (Arizona/Baja) - Wikipedia says Yuma is named after a desert. [WIKI-C]

## 10. Sonora

Rock, hardcore, emerging hip-hop, Latin (e.g. Los Mirlos); The Linda Lindas, Viagra Boys, PUP, Los Bitchos. ## 11. Heineken House

- Heineken has been Coachella's official beer since 2002: 13th year in 2014 (Talents Productions, May 6, 2014 - https://www.talents-productions.com/news/heineken-innovates-at-its-13th-year-of-sponsoring-coachella/); 18th consecutive year in 2019 (PR Newswire, Apr 4, 2019 - https://prnewswire.com/news-releases/the-roots-and-de-la-soul-headline-a-newly-designed-heineken-house-at-coachella-300824798.html). Both count back to 2002.
- Before the House: "Heineken Domes" with DJ lineups and fingerprint-access cold storage for campers (2013). HuffPost - https://www.huffpost.com/entry/coachella-heineken-domes-fingerprints_n_3037200 (not fetched; headline only). PLSN 2013 places Yuma near "the Heineken Dome." [PLSN13]
- 2014: Heineken House replaced the domes - sets by Skrillex, Outkast, Lorde, Girl Talk; Snapchat clues to secret performers; fingerprint beer lockers. [Talents Productions 2014]. Wikipedia: introduced 2014 as a walled, club-like space. [WIKI-C]
- 2019: redesigned with a new structure and location and a first-ever 21+ beer garden; De La Soul (30 years of *3 Feet High and Rising*) and The Roots; Questlove said it was The Roots' second time there. [PR Newswire 2019]. Wikipedia: now an open beer garden with a slanted roof. [WIKI-C]
- 2025: air-conditioned; Pusha T, Afrojack. Cohavit Gil, EDM Identity, Apr 8, 2025 - https://edmidentity.com/2025/04/08/coachella-side-quests-2025

## 12. The Antarctic

- Debuted **2017**, not 2019: a 120-ft geodesic projection dome billed as the largest in the world, built by Obscura with Pacific Domes for Goldenvoice; 15 Christie Boxer projectors (2K, 30k lumens); 108 L-Acoustics speakers on three tiers; 8-minute 360-degree program "Chrysalis"; 500 per show. Live Design (no author/date) - https://www.livedesignonline.com/excellence-installation-awards/antarctic-dome-coachella-2017
- 2019: the 120-ft air-conditioned dome returned as the YouTube 360 dance dome, and the "Antarctic" program was also brought back. Pacific Domes (no date) - https://pacificdomes.com/?p=33210. This is likely the "2019 dome" in the brief.
- Not a desert name - an outlier among stage names. [WIKI-C]

## 13. Despacio

- Built by James Murphy (LCD Soundsystem) and David and Stephen Dewaele (Soulwax/2manydjs) with engineer John Klett; the idea dates to "around May 2010"; a planned weekly Ibiza night never happened. DJ Mag (author/date not shown) - https://djmag.com/content/james-murphy-2manydjs-talk-despacio
- World debut: Manchester International Festival, three vinyl-only nights July 18-20, 2013, in the 1960s ballroom of New Century Hall; "nearly 50,000 watts," about double the Grateful Dead's Wall of Sound; seven 11-ft stacks around the room; 4.5 tons of amplification. McIntosh, Jul 31, 2013 - https://www.mcintoshlabs.com/about/news/Despacio-Powered-by-McIntosh
- Correction: McIntosh made the amps, not the speakers ("McIntosh amplifiers for everything but the subs"); horns/drivers from JBL and Faital; about 10,000 watts per stack excluding subs; "worth just under a million"; vinyl-only because the system "would definitely notice" the difference. [DJ Mag]
- Coachella 2016 (first time): custom tent, seven stacks around a circular lighting rig and a big disco ball, custom Industry Standard analog rotary mixer; on Apr 22, 2016, the day after Prince died, they played "Purple Rain." [MusicTech, Harry Levin, 2026]. Before the festival, VICE wrote that its Coachella use wasn't fully confirmed. Michael Scott Barron, VICE, Jan 10, 2016 - https://www.vice.com/en/article/special-guest-to-join-lcd-soundsystem-at-coachella-a-real-james-murphy-designed-soundsystem/. Wikipedia: small air-conditioned indoor tent, 50,000 watts. [WIKI-C]
- Returned 2023: 3-9 pm daily both weekends, seven stacks, 44 McIntosh amps, about 950 on the floor, target 105 dB, disco ball the only visual. [Groove Cartel, no date]. Return also confirmed by [MusicTech].
- Other stops: This Ain't No Picnic, III Points (2023, 2025), Portola. [MusicTech]

## 14. Spectra

- 2018 art commission by UK studio NEWSUBSTANCE (creative director Patrick O'Mahony): seven-story spiral tower with an observation deck, colors shifting from red to sky blue to hot pink as you climb; about three years from concept to build. urdesign, Apr 19, 2018 - https://www.urdesignmag.com/newsubstance-designs-seven-story-spectra-pavilion-for-coachella-2018/
- Later returns: NEWSUBSTANCE's site has a "returning to California" post (https://www.newsubstance.co.uk/?p=4910, not fetched) - return year unverified.
- An art piece, not a stage.

- 2025: "Desert Sky" (Lot 8) - new campground dance area with DJ sets and the nightly 1 am Silent Disco, Thursday shows camper-only (Chris Lake, Disco Dom named); also "The Turn Down" (late-night downtempo, projection mapping), the Oasis spa (cold plunges, $30 for three minutes). [EDM Identity, Apr 8, 2025]
- "Terrace": in 2013 the "terrace food area" was where Yuma was put [PLSN13]; the 2014 Do LaB moved near "the terrace and food court" [EDMID24]. A food/bar zone, not a stage.
- "Lake Eldorado": no source found in this pass; treat as unverified (see below).

## Unconfirmed / myths

- **"Do LaB started in 2004 at Coachella."** In print (Variety 2023, NPR 2022), but the brothers' own accounts (FADER, Pollstar, EW, LA Weekly) say 2005. 2004 is the company/LIB year.
- **"Do LaB started in 2006."** That's the first year Coachella booked music into it (EDM Identity, FADER) - not its first appearance.
- **Zedd surprise set at Do LaB:** no source found. Zedd's documented 2025 Coachella set (with John Mayer) was on his own billed slot, not Do LaB (Soap Central headlines only, not fetched).
- **Diplo:** confirmed surprise appearances in 2019 (with Major Lazer) and 2022; Diplo + Mau P closed Quasar in 2024 (billed, not a surprise).
- **Skrillex:** confirmed 2017 Do LaB surprise (replacing Kaskade).
- **Quasar "sunrise" sets:** not found. Sets are long (3-4 hours) but end within festival hours; 2024 had a "sunset" b2b.
- **Sonora "2018":** wrong - it debuted 2017.
- **Antarctic "2019 dome":** debuted 2017; the dome came back in 2019 as the YouTube 360 dance dome.
- **Yuma "~2013":** confirmed 2013.
- **"Day Zero" at Coachella:** no link found - Day Zero is Damian Lazarus's Tulum event.
- **"Lake Eldorado" campground stage:** nothing found in this pass. Coachella has used "Eldorado Polo Club" for the Sahara/Quasar area (DJ Mag 2024; WIKI-C), which may be the source of the name. Needs a dedicated search.
- **Sahara ever called something else:** no evidence.
- **Bacardi stage:** no evidence found.
- **Mojave capacity 12-15k:** social-media claim only.
- **LIB 2026 attendance 30,000:** Wikipedia only; unverified.
- **Red Bull Mirage two vs three levels:** sources differ by year.
- **Kanye's 2019 dome demand:** reported via Billboard; never confirmed by West or Goldenvoice.

## Best stories (all sourced above)

1. **The installation that wouldn't leave (2005-2006).** Do LaB's first dome had a water feature so broken the crew showered in it; after the main stage went dark, a thousand people danced there anyway. The next year Tollett overbooked two DJs and parked them in the Flemmings' dome - their "green light." [FADER25] [EW23]
2. **The sign that wasn't allowed (2009).** Coachella ordered Do LaB's sign taken down because the festival had no branding at all back then; the brothers talked Tollett out of it. [FADER25]
3. **Kaskade tweets, Skrillex plays (2017).** Kaskade's secret Do LaB set got out on Twitter, security said the area couldn't hold the crowd, and Skrillex took the slot. [FADER25]
4. **Billie Eilish at a "side" stage (2024).** Over 20,000 people at a stage that started as an art piece with stolen-hours speakers. [FADER25]
5. **Purple Rain on vinyl (2016).** The day after Prince died, Despacio - seven stacks, 50,000 watts, one disco ball - played "Purple Rain." [MusicTech]
6. **A nightclub in a tent (2013).** Yuma opened with wood floors, disco balls, Baroque portraits and air conditioning in the desert; Four Tet improvised the first 90 minutes. [Dancing Astronaut 2013]
7. **Four months to build Quasar (2024).** Heather Shaw heard about it at the end of January and was still building 17 hours before gates; the LED walls run a live Unreal Engine model of the festival grounds. [EW 2024]
8. **The Sahara on permanent footings.** A tent that comes down every year sits on concrete that Goldenvoice digs up each spring; the 2024 rebuild poured about 1,500 tons of it. [LAT23] [Desert Sun 2024]
9. **The dome Kanye wanted (2019).** Reportedly blocked by sewer lines under a toilet bank; Ariana Grande got the slot within a day. [DMN 2019]
10. **Melting vinyl, 1999.** At the first Coachella, Kid Koala's records started to "cone" in 100-plus-degree heat - the backdrop to why water became Do LaB's whole identity. Rob Harvilla, The Ringer, Oct 9, 2019 - https://www.theringer.com/music/2019/10/9/20905896/how-coachella-99-lost-a-million-dollars-started-music-festival-empire; [LAW25]
