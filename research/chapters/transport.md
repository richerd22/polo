# Chapter: Getting There - the Coachella transport system

Research compiled Oct 31, 2026. Each fact is followed by its source(s): publication, date and URL. "Reported by attendees" marks quotes from festivalgoers in news stories; they are not established fact.

## 1. Valley Music Travel (VMT) - who runs the shuttles

- VMT runs the Coachella shuttles. Pass sales and questions go through VMT Guest Services (info@valleymusictravel.com, 1-888-833-1031).
  - Source: VMT Coachella FAQ, coachella.valleymusictravel.com, 2026 season page (accessed Oct 31, 2026) - https://coachella.valleymusictravel.com/faqs
- **Ownership: VMT is not described anywhere as owned by Goldenvoice or AEG.** VMT's own About page calls it "a subsidiary of CID Entertainment." It offers hotel stays, luxury home rentals, bus transport and VIP access in the Coachella Valley.
  - Source: Valley Music Travel, "About Us" (undated, accessed Oct 31, 2026) - https://www.valleymusictravel.com/about-us/
- VMT calls itself the "Official Enhanced Experience Partner of Coachella." It sells black car service and private in-venue transport as add-ons.
  - Source: VMT Platinum Services page (2026 season, accessed Oct 31, 2026) - https://coachella.valleymusictravel.com/platinum-services
- **CID Entertainment's parent:** On Location Experiences acquired CID in October 2018, according to founder Dan Berkowitz. On Location's own site now lists CID as "a division of On Location." On Location is privately held, owned by RedBird Capital Partners, Bruin Sports Capital, 32 Equity and the Carlyle Group.
  - Sources: Pollstar, "Q&A with CID Entertainment's Dan Berkowitz," Jan 29, 2019 - https://news.pollstar.com/2019/01/29/qs-with-a-pollstar-live-panelist-cid-entertainments-dan-berkowitz-on-boutique-festivals-and-destination-events/ ; On Location music "About Us" (undated, accessed Oct 31, 2026) - https://music.onlocationexp.com/about-us
- **CID origin story:** Dan Berkowitz was tour manager for The Disco Biscuits starting in 2004. He started CID ("Consider It Dan") after noticing that most acts didn't offer travel and VIP packages. He founded it in 2007, per Pollstar. In 2008 he sold a small stake for $100,000. As of 2015 it had 60 full-time staff in Philadelphia and fielded about 300 workers at a big festival weekend. Festival producers pay CID a fee to run their fan programs. At Coachella it built 14-by-14-foot safari tents.
  - Sources: Philadelphia Inquirer, "Changing the way fans attend live-music events," Mar 4, 2015 - https://www.inquirer.com/philly/business/20150304_Changing_the_way_fans_attend_live-music_events.html ; Pollstar, Jan 29, 2019 (above)
- **VMT has been the Coachella shuttle partner since at least 2010.** That year Pollstar wrote that "Coachella partnered with Valley Music Travel" for hotel shuttles. 2010 was also the first year all attendees got in-and-out privileges, which is what made hotel shuttles useful.
  - Source: Pollstar, "Coachella Lodging Filling Up," Mar 23, 2010 - http://news.pollstar.com/2010/03/23/coachella-lodging-filling-up/
- An On Location page carries a testimonial from someone who rode CID shuttles at Coachella 2012. Treat that as weak evidence of the CID link going back that far.
  - Source: https://music.onlocationexp.com/about-us
- VMT also ran the shuttles for Goldenvoice's Desert Trip (2016): an "Any Line, Anytime" pass for $25 and an LAX shuttle for $50. Both sold out. The service covered "over 20 stops" and had a shuttle coordinator at every pickup site.
  - Source: Desert Trip, "Shuttles" page (2016, accessed Oct 31, 2026) - https://deserttrip.com/shuttles

### Fleet, drivers, staging (what is documented)
- **Riders:** The City of Indio expected "about 40,000 attendees" to ride shuttle systems across the valley in 2026.
  - Source: NBC Palm Springs, "Coachella 2026 traffic plan…," Apr 7, 2026 - https://www.nbcpalmsprings.com/local-and-community/2026/04/07/coachella-2026-traffic-plan-road-closures-alternate-routes-and-drone-ban-announced-in-indio
- **The drivers are subcontracted.** We know this from the April 2022 food-poisoning outbreak (see Incidents). Reports described the sick drivers as "subcontracted." Their meal was served at the Riverside County Fairgrounds in Indio, and responders treated drivers there, at "the shuttle bus lot," at hotels and at pickup points around the valley.
  - Sources: KESQ, "100+ Coachella shuttle drivers reported being sick…," Apr 26, 2022 - https://kesq.com/news/festival-season/2022/04/26/100-coachella-shuttle-drivers-reported-being-sick-from-food-poisoning/ ; KGTV/Turnto23 (AP/CNS), Apr 25, 2022 - https://www.turnto23.com/news/state/food-poisoning-sickens-coachella-shuttle-bus-drivers
- **Fleet size and driver count:** No public source found. The best floor is that more than 100 drivers reported symptoms in one night in 2022, so the driver pool is well over 100 (inference, not a published count).
- **Shuttle lot:** About a 10-minute walk from the festival entrance, with restrooms at the lot and lines signed by hotel or stop. After the show VMT says it runs "its full fleet." In 2025 FlixBus called Lot 2A "the official shuttle lot," and coachella.com places the Shuttle Lobby on the Yellow Path.
  - Sources: VMT FAQ (above) ; Secret Los Angeles, Apr 8, 2025 - https://secretlosangeles.com/flixbus-direct-service-la-coachella/ ; coachella.com/getting-here (2026, accessed Oct 31, 2026) - https://coachella.com/getting-here
- **Wristband and pass hub:** VMT's will-call is at the Indian Wells Tennis Garden (78200 Miles Ave), which is also a free park-and-ride. NBC Palm Springs called Washington Street near the Tennis Garden "a major congestion point" on Friday as people collect wristbands and board shuttles. In 2026 Indio PD sent overflow drivers there when festival lots filled.
  - Sources: VMT FAQ ; NBC Palm Springs, Apr 10, 2026 - https://www.nbcpalmsprings.com/local-and-community/2026/04/10/traffic-flows-smoothly-into-coachella-delays-expected-in-key-areas ; NBC Palm Springs, Apr 6, 2026 - https://www.nbcpalmsprings.com/local-and-community/2026/04/06/coachella-valley-music-and-arts-festival-traffic-what-to-expect-thursday-through-monday ; NBC Palm Springs, Apr 21, 2026 - https://www.nbcpalmsprings.com/2026/04/21/report-coachella-weekend-two-ends-with-arrests-and-parking-challenges

## 2. Shuttle routes and products

- **Local "Any Line" shuttle:** Stops are about 5 to 25 miles from the Polo Grounds. The pass works from any stop, and not every hotel is a stop. Peak inbound is 2-5 PM and peak outbound is midnight-2 AM. In 2023 inbound ran 12-8 PM, and return service ended 60 minutes after the music.
  - Sources: VMT FAQ (2026) ; VMT "Shuttle Passes 2023" - https://www.valleymusictravel.com/events/coachella-2023/shuttles-2023
- **Typical stop list** (VMT's Stagecoach 2025 list, which matches the Coachella pattern): Hampton Inn Indio, Renaissance Esmeralda and Hyatt Regency Indian Wells, Palm Desert Hampton/Homewood/Courtyard/Residence Inn, La Quinta Resort, Embassy Suites La Quinta, JW Marriott Desert Springs, Omni Rancho Las Palmas, Albertsons Hwy 111, Westin Rancho Mirage, plus three free park-and-rides: Indian Wells Tennis Garden, Agua Caliente Casino and Palm Springs Convention Center. Children 10 and under ride free with an adult passholder.
  - Source: VMT Stagecoach shuttles page - https://valleymusictravel.com/events/stagecoach/shuttles
- **2015 stop list** included Palm Springs hotels (Renaissance, Riviera, Hilton, Saguaro), Shadow Hills RV Resort and Lake Cahuilla campground.
  - Source: VMT Coachella 2015 local hotel shuttles - https://valleymusictravel.com/events/coachella2015/local-hotel-shuttles
- **LAX Shuttle:** Picks up at Terminal 1 baggage claim and runs to hotel stops, with a separate camping-only pass. Riders board Thursday or Friday and return Monday. VMT puts the drive at about 3 hours without traffic and won't cover missed flights.
  - Sources: VMT FAQ ; coachella.com/getting-here
- **Hotel travel packages:** Bundles of hotel, festival pass and Any Line shuttle. The 2022 weekend 2 list had about 20 hotels between 4.4 miles (Residence Inn La Quinta) and 26.4 miles (Hilton Palm Springs) from the festival.
  - Source: VMT Weekend Two Travel Packages 2022 - https://www.valleymusictravel.com/events/coachella-2022/travel-packages-weekend-2-2022/
- **LA, SD and OC "Travel Packages" by coach:** I found no VMT product of this kind. VMT's only documented long-haul shuttle is LAX. Third-party long-haul options include FlixBus in 2025 ($28 one-way from Union Station/El Monte, West LA, USC and UCLA to Lot 2A, downtown Indio and Palm Springs) and Greyhound to Indio station (83-100 Indio Blvd).
  - Sources: Secret Los Angeles, Apr 8, 2025 (above) ; coachella.com/getting-here

## 4. SunLine Transit (public bus)

- Coachella.com lists SunLine only as a phone number, (800) 347-8628, under public transit.
  - Source: coachella.com/getting-here
- SunLine runs 68 buses and 27 paratransit vehicles and carried 2,742,300 riders in 2025. It was founded July 1, 1977, and in 1994 became the first US transit agency to run CNG buses.
  - Source: Wikipedia, "SunLine Transit Agency" (accessed Oct 31, 2026) - https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SunLine_Transit_Agency
- **SunLine has no documented festival service.** Its FY26-28 Short Range Transit Plan (first ~100k characters reviewed) doesn't mention Coachella, Stagecoach or the polo grounds. The festival shuttle system is private (VMT).
  - Source: SunLine SRTP FY26-28 - https://www.sunline.org/images/SRTP_FY_26-28_Board_Approved_w_Page_Numbers.pdf

## 5. Rail: Amtrak, the 2008 Coachella Express, and CV Rail

- **No regular train serves Indio.** Southern Pacific started service in 1876, and Amtrak's Sunset Limited and Texas Eagle stopped there until October 1998, when the station closed for "low ridership."
  - Source: Wikipedia, "Indio station" - https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indio_station
- **The 2008 Coachella Express ran once.** On Thursday, April 24, 2008, a chartered Amtrak train left LA Union Station for a temporary platform in Indio, arriving at 5 PM. It had seven cars, including a Superliner lounge, and was pulled by F59PH No. 458, branded "Coachella Express" and "Get Your Choo-Choo On." About 300 riders went free if they held 3-day camping passes, and got VIP passes on top. DJs played in the cars, including Junkie XL, and Goldenvoice sent a camera crew with host Rosanna Arquette. The trip took about 3 hours. Global Inheritance organized it.
  - Sources: RailPAC, "A Purple Surfliner to Indio," Apr 26, 2008 - https://www.railpac.org/?p=624 ; LA Weekly, "Rolling on the Coachella Express to Indio," Apr 25, 2008 - https://www.laweekly.com/rolling-on-the-coachella-express-to-indio/ ; Grist, May 7, 2008 - https://grist.org/?p=23231 ; BizBash, Apr 1, 2008 - https://www.bizbash.com/event-production-planning/amtrak-to-partner-with-coachella-fest-on-attendee-shuttle-from-l-a- ; Global Inheritance transportation programs - https://www.globalinheritance.org/programs/transportation
- **The 2016 "Choo-Choo-Chella" plan:** RCTC floated festival trains for 2017-18 with a budget of about $1.2M. They never ran.
  - Source: EDMTunes, May 4, 2016 - https://www.edmtunes.com/2016/05/aboard-choo-choo-chella-train/
- **The 2019 special-event platform was funded, then canceled.** CalSTA awarded RCTC $5.9M in State Rail Assistance money (gas tax) and RCTC added $2.7M, for $8.6M total. That would have paid for a 900-ft temporary platform at the Indio Transit Center, with trains from LA Union Station "as early as 2020."
  - Sources: RCTC "The Point," Mar 12, 2019 - https://www.rctc.org/choo-choo-funding/ ; KVCR, Mar 7, 2019 - https://www.kvcrnews.org/local-transportation/2019-03-07/funding-secured-to-allow-amtrak-special-event-trains-from-la-to-the-coachella-and-stagecoach-fests
  - Canceled in 2020 during COVID "after arrangements with Union Pacific could not be resolved." (Wikipedia, "Indio station," above. Needs a primary source.)
- **Coachella Valley Rail today:** On Jan 30, 2026, RCTC awarded HDR an $80M contract for the Tier 2 environmental review. The corridor runs about 144 miles from LA with 9 proposed stops. The project will likely exceed $1.5B, and service could start "by 2040." Earlier RCTC material proposed two daily round trips.
  - Sources: NBC Palm Springs, Jan 30, 2026 - https://www.nbcpalmsprings.com/local-and-community/2026/01/30/riverside-county-transportation-commission-awards-80m-for-cv-rail-study ; RCTC, Mar 12, 2019 (above)

## 6. Air: PSP, Thermal (TRM), private jets and helicopters

- **Thermal / Jacqueline Cochran Regional (TRM):** Owned by Riverside County, with an 8,500-ft main runway. It was a WWII Army airfield built in 1942, then Thermal Airport (1948-98) and Desert Resorts Regional, renamed in 2004. It logged 57,093 operations (about 156 a day) in the 12 months to Apr 30, 2023, with 18 based jets. Its Wikipedia entry doesn't mention Coachella; the busy season it describes is the equestrian Desert Circuit, December to March.
  - Source: Wikipedia, "Jacqueline Cochran Regional Airport" - https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacqueline_Cochran_Regional_Airport
- **Semi-private pop-ups into TRM:**
  - JetSuiteX (around 2018), Burbank to Thermal in about 35 min, from $199 each way. Three flights on Friday of W1, two on W2.
    - Source: Robb Report - https://robbreport.com/motors/aviation/fly-semi-privately-to-the-coachella-valley-music-and-arts-festival-2787799/
  - 2019: JetSuiteX Oakland to Thermal from $748 (W1) and $898 (W2).
    - Source: SF Chronicle (2019 festival dates) - https://preview-prod.w.sfchronicle.com/travel/article/Palm-Springs-airfares-soar-this-spring-13706496.php
  - Aero 2024, Van Nuys to TRM, seats from $975.
    - Source: Private Jet Card Comparisons, Mar 28, 2024 - https://privatejetcardcomparisons.com/2024/03/28/aero-pulls-out-of-dallas-adds-coachella-pop-up/
  - JSX 2025, Burbank to TRM, Apr 10-14, from $399.
    - Source: PJCC, Mar 4, 2025 - https://privatejetcardcomparisons.com/2025/03/04/jsx-offering-pop-up-flights-timed-for-coachella-festival/
  - JSX 2026, Santa Monica to TRM, Apr 9 out and Apr 13 back, $606 each way, about 50 min.

WingX weekly data for Coachella week 2026 (week 15) is statewide only: 5,426 California private-jet departures, up 9% year over year.
  - Sources: PJCC, Apr 14, 2017 - https://privatejetcardcomparisons.com/2017/04/14/coachella-attracts-the-private-jet-set ; PJCC, Apr 16, 2026 - https://privatejetcardcomparisons.com/2026/04/16/private-jet-flight-activity-analysis-2026-week-15/

  - Source: Fortune, Mar 25, 2016 - https://fortune.com/2016/03/25/uber-blade-coachella-helicopter

Its busiest month ever was March 2025, with nearly 500,000 passengers; that is not Coachella month. Airfare spikes: in 2019, SFO-PSP round trips ran about $600-800 for W1 and $500-600 for W2, against about $320 in early April.
  - Sources: Palm Springs Post, Jan 20, 2026 - https://thepalmspringspost.com/palm-springs-international-airport-reports-record-3-3-million-passengers-in-2025/ ; SF Chronicle (above)

  - Source: NBC Palm Springs, Apr 7, 2026 (above)

## 7. The road plan

- **Pre-festival closure:** Avenue 50 between Monroe and Madison closes for about six weeks, through both Coachella weekends and Stagecoach. It ran Mar 25 to May 6 in 2024 and Mar 23 to May 4 in 2026. Jim Curtis, Indio's community services manager, called it "essential to ensure the safety of workers, residents, and commuters alike."
  - Sources: Desert Sun via PressReader, Apr 12, 2024 - https://www.pressreader.com/usa/the-desert-sun/20240412/281509346220618 ; NBC Palm Springs, Mar 23, 2026 - https://www.nbcpalmsprings.com/local-and-community/2026/03/23/know-before-you-go-festival-season-begins-avenue-50-closes-valleywide-traffic-expected-through-may
- **Weekend closures (Fri-Mon)** have been the same list since at least 2019: Avenue 49 (Hjorth to Monroe), Avenue 50 (Madison to Jackson), Hjorth St (Ave 49 to 50), and Madison St (Ave 49 to 52).
  - Sources: KFI/CNS, Apr 18, 2019 (above) ; NBC Palm Springs, Mar 27, 2026 - https://www.nbcpalmsprings.com/2026/03/27/indio-releases-festival-season-road-closure-plan-for-coachella-and-stagecoach-weekends
- **Choke points:** Southbound Jefferson, Washington and Monroe from I-10 to Ave 52; Avenue 48; Hwy 111 at Jefferson and Monroe; and the eastbound I-10 exits. Peak inbound is about 3-8 PM and outbound about midnight-3 AM.
  - Sources: NBC Palm Springs, Mar 27, 2026 ; NBC Palm Springs, Apr 6, 2026 (above)
- **Freeways (Caltrans):** I-10 (Beaumont to Indio) and SR-60 (Moreno Valley to Beaumont) are the pressure points, with eastbound peaking Friday and westbound Sunday. Caltrans spokeswoman Terri Kasinga: "Avoid delays by leaving early mornings on those peak travel days." The 2019 plan assumed capacity crowds of 125,000 a day.
  - Source: KVCR, Apr 9, 2019 - https://www.kvcrnews.org/local-transportation/2019-04-09/caltrans-warns-inland-empire-prepare-for-coachella-traffic
- **Camper exodus (Monday):** About 40,000 campers leave each Monday (2019, 2024 and 2026 figures). Camper gates open around 8:30-9 AM, and outbound traffic generally clears by about 11:30 AM. Drivers heading west on I-10 are told to get on before 9 AM.
  - Sources: KVCR 2019 ; Desert Sun 2024 ; NBC Palm Springs, Apr 7 and Apr 6, 2026 (above)
- **Traffic management:** CV Sync (cv-sync.com) is the regional traffic program; Kris Gunterson is CV Sync Traffic Program Manager.
  - Source: NBC Palm Springs, Apr 6, 2026
- **Policing:** Indio PD's Traffic Division handles festival traffic enforcement (Sgt. Jesse Yuhas, 2025). CHP raised patrols valley-wide; Indio CHP PIO David Torres in 2025: "We do have more officers on the road but are also more hyper-aware looking for violations." Arrests and citations by weekend:
  - W2 2025: 112 arrests and 83 placard citations
  - W2 2026: 106 arrests and 87 disabled-placard citations
  - Sources: NBC Palm Springs, Apr 16, 2025 - https://www.nbcpalmsprings.com/local-and-community/2025/04/16/indio-police-crack-down-festival-driving ; KESQ, Apr 10, 2025 - https://kesq.com/?p=1660977 ; NBC Palm Springs, Apr 21, 2026 (above)
- **Lots and paths (2026):**
  - Rideshare is at Lot 1D (Ave 49 & Monroe), open Fri-Sun 11 AM-3 AM, with the longest waits from midnight to 2:30 AM. In 2023 Uber added a $20 event surcharge for pickups from 10 PM to 3 AM, all of it paid to the driver.
  - Friend and taxi drop-off is at Lot 13 (Ave 52 & Madison).
  - Thursday pickup and drop-off uses Lot 2A.
  - Walk and bike entries: Red (Ave 50/Madison), Green (Ave 52/Madison), Yellow (Ave 49/Monroe) and Blue (Ave 52/Monroe).
  - No pedestrians on Monroe between Aves 49 and 52, or on Madison between 50 and 52.
  - Bikes locked anywhere but racks "will be removed and forfeited."
  - Day parking is not guaranteed, and "Cars left after 2am will be towed." No RVs or golf carts.
  - Sources: coachella.com/getting-here (2026) ; Uber blog, Apr 7, 2023 - https://www.uber.com/blog/palm-springs/ride-coachellafestival23
- **Carpoolchella:** A Global Inheritance program run with Coachella. Cars with 4 or more people that are decorated and spotted entering can win backstage passes, VIP upgrades or "VIP tickets for life." Start year is not confirmed on the source page.
  - Sources: coachella.com/getting-here ; Global Inheritance - https://www.globalinheritance.org/programs/transportation

## 8. Incidents

- **2025 camping gridlock:** On Thursday, Apr 10, 2025, campers sat in line for 8 to 12 or more hours. Attendee reports (KESQ) included arriving at 3 AM and still waiting near 11 AM, one person "Seven hours into this," and "I've seen people pass out through heat exhaustion." Patch reported people relieving themselves at the roadside with no restrooms.
  - On Apr 15, 2025, Goldenvoice SVP of Public Safety George Cunningham told La Quinta City Council that organizers hadn't anticipated the early arrivals: "Again, [we] apologize for what took place to your residents, the impact that it had to the community."
  - The fix was more toll and security booths for W2. For 2026, Indio described it as a second toll-booth entry with a new lane on the north side of the venue, alongside the existing south entry.
  - Sources: KESQ, Apr 10, 2025 - https://kesq.com/?p=1660977 ; Patch, Apr 16, 2025 - https://patch.com/california/palmdesert/coachella-apologizes-unacceptable-chaotic-traffic-lines ; NBC Palm Springs, Apr 16, 2025 - https://www.nbcpalmsprings.com/2025/04/16/coachella-organizers-apologize-for-12hour-traffic-delays-promise-fixes-for-weekend-two ; NBC Palm Springs, Apr 17, 2025 - https://www.nbcpalmsprings.com/2025/04/17/coachella-weekend-2-is-here-will-the-traffic-be-better ; NBC Palm Springs, Apr 6, 2026 (above)
- **2022 shuttle driver food poisoning:** After a catered Sunday-night meal on Apr 24, 2022 (pasta and pre-packaged salad) at the county fairgrounds in Indio, more than 100 subcontracted drivers reported symptoms. Forty-six were assessed at hospitals: Eisenhower 23, Desert Regional 12, JFK 11. Calls began shortly after midnight on Monday, Apr 25. The caterer was never publicly named in these reports, and no attendees were affected. Early reports said 24, then 40; county health later said more than 100.
  - Sources: KESQ, Apr 26, 2022 (above) ; NBC Palm Springs, Apr 26, 2022 - https://www.nbcpalmsprings.com/news/2022/04/26/probe-continues-into-apparent-food-poisoning-of-coachella-fest-drivers ; Food Poison Journal, Apr 28, 2022 - https://www.foodpoisonjournal.com/foodborne-illness-outbreaks/over-100-coachella-bus-drivers-sickened-by-mystery-illness-from-mystery-catering-company/
- **2019:** No 2019 "traffic jam" incident found. Coverage was routine Caltrans and Indio advisories, and MyNewsLA reported "Desert freeways all clear" on W2 Friday morning (Apr 19, 2019). The famous long-line incident is 2025.

## Best stories
1. **The 2008 Coachella Express:** A one-time Amtrak charter with DJs in the cars, Junkie XL, free ice cream and beer, and Rosanna Arquette with a Goldenvoice camera crew. Riders got free VIP passes.
