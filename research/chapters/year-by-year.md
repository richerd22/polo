# Coachella year by year - companion notes

Compiled Oct 31, 2026. The full sourced records are in year-by-year.json. Below are the best stories, the corrections to the brief, and what is still unconfirmed.

## Best stories

1. **Ticket prices, 1999 to 2026.** A day ticket cost $50 in 1999 (Ringer). By 2026, GA started at $649 for weekend 1 (California Today). The two-day pass went from $142 in 2004 (Las Vegas Sun) to a $249 three-day pass in 2007 (JamBase), then $269 from 2008 to 2011. The two-weekend era runs $285 (2012), $349 (2013), $375 (2015), $399 (2016-17), $429 (2018-19), $449 (2022), $499 (2024) and $549-649 (2025-26).
2. **The gross curve, from Pollstar and Billboard.** 2013: $67.2M. 2014: $78.3M. 2015: $84.26M (198,000 tickets). 2016: $94.2M (198,000). 2017: $114.59M (250,000), the first recurring festival over $100M. No gross has been published since 2017.
3. **2010 was the breaking point.** It was the first three-day-pass-only year, with 75,000 passes sold out. LA Weekly reported gate-crashing, fistfights, counterfeit tickets, three-hour entry lines and a near-crush at an exit. The volcanic ash cloud stranded seven acts. Tollett's 2011 fixes were a wrought-iron fence, a new road lane and the end of wristband exchange. On May 31, 2011 he announced the second weekend.
4. **The 2007 Rage reunion.** Coachella went to three days for the first time. Day three sold out instantly, and the campground rioted afterward. Stagecoach launched the same spring.
5. **Hidden firsts.** The Sahara tent already had a laser show in 2002 (Daily Bruin). Camping began in 2003. YouTube streamed three stages to almost 4 million viewers in 2011. Both weekends were first streamed in 2019. The La Grande XL Ferris wheel debuted in 2017, replacing an earlier wheel that was on site by 2010.
6. **2023's headliner slate.** Bad Bunny, BLACKPINK and Frank Ocean were the first Latin headliner, the first Asian headliner and the first all-nonwhite slate. That same year, weekend 2 failed to sell out for the first time since 2012.
7. **The slump and the rebound, 2024-26.** In 2024 weekend 1 took 27 days to sell out, and Stagecoach outsold each Coachella weekend. In 2025 weekend 2 passes resold at half of face value. In 2026 everything sold out in about three days, and Bieber reportedly got more than $10M.

## Corrections to the brief

- **2006 Madonna:** her Sahara-tent set was short, about six songs, and ran 20 minutes late to boos. Billboard filed it under "disappointments." It was not a triumph.
- **2010 "crush":** this was overcrowding, gate-crashing and one near-crush at an exit. No casualty event was reported.
- **2012 GA price:** it was $285 plus fees, not the $315 implied by Wikipedia's arithmetic.
- **2014 attendance:** the 579,000 figure is not a typo. It counts daily admissions (96,500 x 6).
- **2019:** Kanye's dome demand cost him the 2019 headline slot, so Sunday Service was the consolation, not a booking. Lead rigger Christopher Griffin died during setup.
- **2022:** Ye's slot went to Swedish House Mafia plus The Weeknd. Rage Against the Machine and Travis Scott, carried over from 2020, also did not play.
- **2023:** Blink-182 replaced Frank Ocean on weekend 2 only.
- **2026 headliners:** Sabrina Carpenter (Friday, with Anyma late), Justin Bieber (Saturday) and Karol G (Sunday).

## Unconfirmed / needs a primary source

- **1999:** attendance (37,000 total) and loss ($850K vs "about $1M") differ by source.
- **2001-2003:** the $65 price, 32,000 attendance, 2,252 campsites and the 2002 Siouxsie reunion are from Wikipedia only.
- **2006:** ticket price not found.
- **2006-2012:** grosses come from Wikipedia's table, not checked against Billboard Boxscore. The Pollstar 2012-2014 year-end PDFs were not found.
- **2008:** "first non-sellout" and "lost money" are Wikipedia only.
- **2014:** the "VIP over $5,000" figure is probably a premium package. Base VIP price not found.
- **2018-2023:** attendance is not reported anywhere. Treat 125,000 per day as the permitted cap, not actual attendance.
- **2021:** I don't have a primary source for the cancellation date.
- **2022:** I can't confirm that Billie Eilish was the "youngest headliner." Variety's 100,000-plus crowd for Styles was seen only as a Wikipedia citation.
- **2023:** the $4M ice-rink loss is Wikipedia's citation of Billboard and was not seen directly.
- **2024:** the KESQ ticket counts (about 81,000 per weekend) may cover city-taxed tickets only.
- **2026:** the gross is unknown. Ignore Latin Post's $220-240M. Bieber's $10M+ comes from one unnamed source (Rolling Stone).
