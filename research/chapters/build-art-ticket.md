## THE BUILD

### Timeline and crews
- AEG's 2025 stagehand posting for Coachella and Stagecoach asked for availability from March 17 to May 9, 2025, with the expected work period March 30 to May 3. It listed day, mid and overnight shifts at $20-25, with the unit not stated. Duties included staging, truck loading, scenery rigging, and helping with audio, video and lighting. - https://builtin.com/job/stagehands-coachella-stagecoach-2025/4226728 - Built In (AEG job posting) - removed Mar 26, 2025
  - Inferred: in 2025 the stagehand window ran about 5 weeks and covered both Coachella weekends plus Stagecoach (Apr 25-27, 2025).
- Audio: gear prep starts about two weeks out, and trucks start arriving on site about a week before the festival. Rat Sound had 65-70 people working the festival and rented 25-30 local houses for crew. - https://fohonline.com/articles/production-profile/the-2018-coachella-valley-music-and-arts-festival/ - FOH magazine (George Petersen) - Jun 6, 2018
- Rat Sound's audio crew arrives about 4 days before the show, and overall audio planning takes about two months. - https://soundgirls.org/?p=2167 - SoundGirls.org (Q&A with Rat Sound) - Apr 4, 2014
- Rat Sound has handled audio for 18 of Coachella's first 19 years (as of 2018). The system was mostly L-Acoustics, about 847 main cabinets not counting sidefills and wedges, and the Main Stage field alone had 168 K2. - FOH, Jun 6, 2018 (above)
- In the desert wind, PA and delay clusters are lowered and tied off at night or in high wind. - FOH 2018; SoundGirls 2014 (above)
- The new 2024 Sahara: construction started December 2023. About 156 truckloads of concrete (1,500 tons) were poured for underground footings, and crews worked overnight to keep Indio's streets clear. - https://www.yahoo.com/entertainment/coachella-2024-sahara-tent-moving-003831144.html - The Desert Sun (Ema Sasic) via Yahoo - Feb 6-7, 2024
- Gallagher Staging was set to build the main stage for the 2020 festival, which was postponed. Choura Events is named alongside it as a builder of tents, staging and facilities for festivals like Coachella. - https://www.nme.com/news/music/stage-construction-production-teams-coachella-building-coronavirus-triage-tents-2638799 - NME (Sam Moore) - Apr 1, 2020
- A Goldenvoice lead rigger with 20 years on the Coachella team died in a fall during stage setup on Apr 6, 2019. Indio PD called it an "industrial accident," and OSHA led the investigation. - https://www.foxnews.com/us/coachella-stagehand-dies-during-setup-police-say - Fox News (Paulina Dedaj) - Apr 6, 2019
- Crews built a grass hill from scratch between the 2019 weekends for Kanye West's Easter Sunday Service. Dirt went in Tuesday and sod Thursday and Friday. Paul Tollett's brother Perry designed the hidden loudspeakers. Tollett's first real day off came after Coachella weekend 2, and then he went back to work for Stagecoach. - https://www.pressreader.com/usa/los-angeles-times/20190423/282007558797522 - Los Angeles Times (via PressReader) - Apr 23, 2019
- More than 10,000 temporary festival-linked jobs, and $700M in combined economic activity from Coachella and Stagecoach, according to a report from Gov. Newsom's office. - https://www.nbcpalmsprings.com/local-and-community/2026/04/13/coachella-and-stagecoach-generate-700-million-boost-indio-economy-and-jobs - NBC Palm Springs - Apr 13, 2026

### Capacity and site
- Indio's City Council unanimously approved raising Coachella's cap from 99,000 to 125,000 and Stagecoach's from 75,000 to 85,000, phased in. - https://www.hypebot.com/when-is-a-festival-too-big-coachella-stagecoach-to-expand/ - Hypebot - Apr 20, 2016
- In 2013 Indio agreed to host Coachella and Stagecoach through 2030 (citing the LA Times, 2013). Goldenvoice bought 280 acres around the Empire Polo Club (citing the LA Times, 2012). - https://www.yahoo.com/entertainment/33-surprising-facts-secrets-coachella-021602366.html - BuzzFeed via Yahoo - Apr 18, 2023
- On-site camping holds roughly 35,000 people. - https://coachella.com/sustainability - Coachella.com (official) - accessed Oct 31, 2026

### Power
- Official claims: generators run on renewable diesel, solar panels generate power on site, grid power is used "where available," and power is "hybridized." The Do LaB stage, new in 2025, runs on zero-emissions battery systems. - https://coachella.com/sustainability - Coachella.com - accessed Oct 31, 2026
- The local utility is the Imperial Irrigation District (IID), not Southern California Edison. IID's service area includes "Coachella Valley cities including Mecca, Thermal, La Quinta, Coachella and Indio." - https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Imperial_Irrigation_District - Wikipedia - accessed Oct 31, 2026
  - I found no source that gives the festival's grid draw, its generator count, or a dedicated IID service to the polo grounds. See Unconfirmed.
- In 2024 PRG supplied power distribution (plus lighting) for J Balvin's main-stage set. - https://www.prg.com/en/news/prg-provides-production-support-for-j-balvin-doja-cat-anti-up-and-more-at-coachella-2024 - PRG (vendor press release) - May 23, 2024

### Water
- The festival doubled its spigots across the venue and campgrounds for refills. Single-use plastic water bottles were replaced with aluminum, except in some back-of-house and stage safety areas. The polo fields are irrigated with non-potable water, and some turf is being replaced with alternative landscaping. - https://coachella.com/sustainability - Coachella.com - accessed Oct 31, 2026
- There are free "twice-filtered" water refill stations throughout the venue and campgrounds. An empty, refillable, non-metal bottle is allowed. - https://www.coachella.com/faq - Coachella.com FAQ (2026/27 edition) - accessed Oct 31, 2026
- Waste (Coachella and Stagecoach combined, 2024): 616,278 lb composted or recycled, 79,120 lb donated to the Galilee Center, and 56,051 lb of food to the Coachella Valley Rescue Mission. - coachella.com/sustainability (above). The Galilee and Rescue Mission figures are corroborated, with small differences, by https://www.palmspringslife.com/arts-culture/inside-the-sustainability-efforts-of-coachella-festival-and-stagecoach/ - Palm Springs Life - undated (covers 2024)

### Stages and production vendors
- **Sahara (2024 rebuild):** Goldenvoice built a new steel structure from scratch. Elation's account gives about 400 x 410 ft, nearly 100 ft tall, with 16 ribs, about 40 ft wider than the old tent. Four headliner-level acts played per day, with changeovers as short as 10-15 minutes. Production and lighting design were by Todd Roberts (Production Design Works), with Benjamin Van Brande on CAD and renders. The rig used 956 Elation fixtures from Aspect Lighting. Main Stage and Quasar fixtures came from 4Wall. - https://www.elationlighting.eu/blog/1300-elation-lights-dazzle-coachella-2024 - Elation Professional (vendor) - undated, covers 2024
- The Desert Sun gives the new Sahara as 320 ft long, built in steel instead of aluminum, with "more weight capacity and production elements" (Jason Brown, Goldenvoice). Its new site is between Avenue 51 and Polo Road. The tent sat near the Rose Garden until 2018, then moved to the Eldorado Polo Club side. - Desert Sun via Yahoo, Feb 2024 (above)
- The 2018 Sahara was described as 300 x 300 ft. - FOH 2018 (above)
- **No Tait credit found for the Sahara.** Elation and the Desert Sun credit Goldenvoice, Todd Roberts and PDW. Don't print "Tait designed the Sahara" without a source.
- **Main Stage audio (2018):** 8 K1SB flown subs per side, 12 K1 over 4 K2 (inner) and 12 K1 over 2 K2 (outer) per side, plus delay towers. - FOH 2018 (above)
- **Main Stage lighting (2024):** nearly 200 Elation fixtures, plus about 130 on the Quasar stage. - Elation (above)
- 2026 headliner rigs came from vendors such as PRG, Robe and others (examples: Doja Cat, Lady Gaga). The headliners bring their own production. - PRG May 2024 (above); https://old.robe.cz/news/robe-taps-into-lady-gaga-mayhem-at-coachella - Robe - 2025
- I found no published main-stage dimensions (width or height). See Unconfirmed.

### The Do LaB
- Founded by the Flemming brothers (twins Josh and Jesse, and Dede). Josh does design, Jesse music and Dede logistics. The name comes from their old LA warehouse, a former meth lab. In 2004 they snuck in speakers after hours and people danced in their dome. The 2022 stage was a 200 x 200 ft tension-fabric tent. NPR calls it a "small, independent, family-run business." - https://www.npr.org/2022/04/22/1093356468/coachellas-music-fan-fave-do-lab-is-back - NPR - Apr 22, 2022
- Do LaB books its own lineups, which are "entirely separate from Coachella," absent from the official set-time grid, and listed on the flyer only as an art installation. Lineups are announced on Do LaB's own channels in late March. They got the green light "a week or two before" the 2004 festival. Past surprise guests include Skrillex, Major Lazer, Richie Hawtin and John Summit. - https://malaysia.news.yahoo.com/coachella-lab-tent-unpublished-lineups-200619276.html - Variety (Lily Moayeri) via Yahoo - Apr 13, 2023
- Stage timeline: 2010, first permanent central home; 2014, first open-concept design; 2015-16 "Big Fish"; 2017-18 "The Beacon" (with Heather Shaw / Vita Modus), restored hours before W2 Friday after windstorm damage; 2019-2023 "Warrior One" (scarab-inspired). - https://edmidentity.com/2024/04/08/do-lab-coachella-history - EDM Identity (Logan Garrison) - Apr 8, 2024
- The Do LaB has curated its own area since 2004. - https://lamag.com/news/coachellas-monumental-artwork-sets-festival-apart - LAmag (Steve Appleford) - Apr 21, 2023
- The 2025 Do LaB stage is battery-powered. - coachella.com/sustainability (above)

### Sound limits and curfew
- The curfew is 1 a.m. Friday and Saturday and midnight Sunday. It is part of Goldenvoice's contract with Indio (BuzzFeed citing the Desert Sun, 2014). - Hypebeast below; BuzzFeed/Yahoo 2023 (above)
- Fine structure: $20,000 once a set runs 5 minutes over, then $1,000 per minute after that. Fines go to Indio's General Fund. - https://hypebeast.com/2023/4/coachella-117000-usd-breaking-curfew-weekend-1-news-info - Hypebeast (Aaron Chow) - Apr 17, 2023
- 2023 W1: $117,000 total, with sets running over on every night (Frank Ocean ran 25 minutes over). - Hypebeast (above). 2023 overall: more than $168,000 (Bad Bunny, Frank Ocean, The Weeknd). - WMMO below
- 2026 W2: $44,000. Justin Bieber went 2 minutes over ($20,000) and Anyma 9 minutes over ($24,000), per TMZ. Paul McCartney in 2009 ran 54 minutes over, for $54,000. - https://www.wmmo.com/news/trending/coachella-fined-over-justin-bieber-anyma-sets-go-past-curfew/ICB3UBTU6NBI5EN52FWWJSIVGI/ - WMMO (citing TMZ) - Apr 22, 2026
- I found no published dB limit. See Unconfirmed.

### Cell coverage, Wi-Fi, app
- AT&T (about 2016): a "Drum Set" antenna with about 30x the capacity of a single-beam antenna and more than 2x its earlier "Cheese Wheel," plus 4 COWs, for 59 beams total. AT&T customers used 29.2 TB over two weekends, a claimed music-festival record, up from about 12.2 TB in 2015 and 6.5 TB two years earlier. Antenna beam counts grew from 2 (2011) to 5 (2012) to 9 and then 18 (2013-14). - https://www.fierce-network.com/tech/at-t-debuts-drum-set-antenna-at-coachella - Fierce Network - undated (content indicates 2016)
- AT&T 2013: the 18-beam antenna and at least three COWs peaked at about 83 GB per hour, with 6,054 GB over two weekends. - https://9to5mac.com/2013/05/19/inside-atts-83gbhour-mobile-cell-tower-or-why-your-iphone-no-longer-drops-out-at-huge-events/ - 9to5Mac (Jared Weintraub) - May 19, 2013
- T-Mobile 2026: "multiple" COWs (no count given), the SON network, and T-Satellite messaging as a fallback. The same setup stayed for Stagecoach. - https://www.t-mobile.com/news/network/t-mobile-gets-coachella-ready - T-Mobile Newsroom - Apr 10, 2026
- The official app handles wristband registration (the only way to register), set times, maps and lost and found. The FAQ does not mention Wi-Fi. - https://www.coachella.com/faq - Coachella.com - accessed Oct 31, 2026
- The livestream has its own separate "Coachella Livestream" app (iOS and Android). - YouTube Blog, Apr 2, 2025 (above)

### Teardown and the flip to Stagecoach
- What carries over: the Ferris wheel stays in place, and Spectra stays as a permanent installation. Stagecoach's Mane Stage is rotated 90 degrees clockwise from Coachella's main stage. The Rose Garden and the Sahara are not used. Most large art leaves. - https://www.yahoo.com/entertainment/differences-similarities-between-coachella-stagecoach-232121095.html - Los Angeles Times (Vanessa Franko) via Yahoo - Apr 26, 2024
- Renames for Stagecoach: Mojave becomes Palomino; Yuma becomes Diplo's Honkytonk; Sonora becomes the air-conditioned Bud Light Backyard; Gobi becomes Yellowstone Dutton Ranch; the craft beer barn is renamed for Guy Fieri. - LA Times, Apr 26, 2024 (above)
- Stagecoach audio (2014): the Main Stage moves to the former Outdoor Theatre site, and half the Sahara gear is removed. - SoundGirls, Apr 4, 2014 (above)
- Rescue Mission and Galilee operations run about five weeks around the festivals, sweeping the grounds Monday mornings and collecting VIP leftovers Mondays and Tuesdays. - Palm Springs Life (above)

## THE ART

### People and process
- **Paul Clemente:** main art curator since 2007, after earlier volunteering (BuzzFeed citing the LA Times, 2019). He spent 18 years in Hollywood effects (miniatures and rigging on *Titanic* and *The Matrix*) and leads the in-house fabricators. - LAmag, Apr 21, 2023 (above); BuzzFeed/Yahoo, Apr 18, 2023 (above)
- In 2013 PBS SoCal called Clemente "art director for eight years." He built *Mirage*, a 3-story mid-century house on the polo field with 23 projectors. Goldenvoice received about 300 proposals for 2013, a record. The festival funds the selected projects fully. Clemente's criteria: the "largest, most dramatic" pieces that can be built in ten days. - https://pbssocal.org/shows/artbound/beyond-the-snail-the-art-and-imagination-of-the-coachella-festival - PBS SoCal *Artbound* (Benjamin Gottlieb) - Apr 24, 2013
- Early art was borrowed from Burning Man. Later came an open call (one year drew more than 200 proposals), and the team now approaches artists directly. Most large work is built in a 20,000 sq ft tent near the grounds, though Spectra was built in the UK. Work on the 2023 pieces started the previous summer. - LAmag, Apr 21, 2023 (above)
- **Raffi Lehrer:** in 2019 he was associate art director at Goldenvoice, choosing designers and making sure structures could take crowds. - https://www.kcrw.com/shows/design-and-architecture/stories/coachella-commission-a-dream-come-true-for-artist-sofia-enriquez - KCRW (Frances Anderton) - Apr 16, 2019
- He is now founder of **Public Art Company (PAC)**, which curates the program with Clemente (credited in 2024, 2025 and 2026). - https://www.dezeen.com/2024/04/15/coachella-2024-three-large-scale-and-deeply-interactive-installations/amp/ - Dezeen - Apr 15, 2024; https://www.archdaily.com/1040623/coachella-2026-immersive-installations-explore-monumentality-and-light-transparency-in-the-california-desert - ArchDaily (Antonia Piñeiro) - Apr 14, 2026
  - The user's brief said "Art Collective Goldenvoice." The sources say Public Art Company.
- The program took its current multidisciplinary form in 2016, when production moved mostly in-house and on site, built by local carpenters, metalworkers, painters and riggers. Installations are developed over 2-3 years. Material reuse is a priority, and designs are increasingly modular so they can live on as public art. Example: Francis Kéré's *Sarbalé ke*, now in a park in Indio. - https://www.iconeye.com/design/public-art-company-art-commissions-coachella-2025 - ICON Magazine - Apr 16, 2025

### Year by year (selected)
- **2013** - *Helix Poeticus* (Poetic Kinetics; Cynthia Washburn and Patrick Shearn): the snail, about 30 ft tall and 80 ft long, with iridescent silver skin. It was built on a festival variable-reach forklift and covered in graffiti and mural art, from a concept first developed at Burning Man in 2002. The same year had a praying mantis piece; the team spent about two months on the two. - PBS SoCal, Apr 24, 2013 (above)
- **2014** - *Escape Velocity* (Poetic Kinetics): a 36 ft tall, 57 ft long, 40 ft wide astronaut of steel tubing, chicken wire and pin rods, riding a variable-reach forklift through the crowd. It "was to be sold" after the festival. - https://www.complex.com/style/a/susan-cheng/the-backstory-of-the-massive-astronaut-drifting-through-coachella-festival-this-year - Complex (Susan Cheng) - Apr 15, 2014
- **2018** - *Spectra* (NEWSUBSTANCE / Patrick O'Mahony): a 7-story spiral tower with an observation deck, one of six studios commissioned that year. - LAmag 2023 (above); https://newsubstance.co.uk/journal/spectra-coachella - NEWSUBSTANCE (studio) - undated
- **2019** - *Overview Effect* (Poetic Kinetics): the astronaut returns, about 45 ft crouched, in a "burnt from reentry" suit. The same year had Kéré's *Sarbalé ke*, Sofia Enríquez's *Mismo* (6 wooden paisley murals), and Spectra's return as "one of the few" pieces back for a second year. - https://www.timeout.com/los-angeles/news/the-big-astronaut-is-back-to-hover-over-coachellaand-its-looking-a-little-burnt-out-041219 - Time Out LA (Michael Juliano) - Apr 12, 2019; KCRW, Apr 16, 2019 (above)
- **2023** - *The Messengers* (Kumkum Fernando, three statues about 75 ft tall); *Molecular Cloud* (Vincent Leroy); *Eden* (Maggie West); *Holoflux* (Güvenç Özel). - LAmag, Apr 21, 2023
- **2024** - *Monarchs: A House in Six Parts* (HANNAH: plywood towers 35-71 ft on 3D-printed concrete bases); *Babylon* (Nebbia, 60 ft cube); *Dancing in the Sky* (Morag Myerscough). - Dezeen, Apr 15, 2024
- **2025** - *Take Flight* (Isabel + Helen, a 60 ft kinetic turbine piece); *Le Grand Bouquet* (Uchronia, inflatable flowers); *Taffy* (Stephanie Lin). - ICON, Apr 16, 2025
- **2026** - *Maze* (Sabine Marcelis, inflatable PVC); *Starry Eyes* (Kyriakos Chatziparaskevas, cactus towers nearly 40 ft); *Visage Brut* (LADG with Stud-IO Construction); returning, *Network Operations* (Dedo Vabo's Hippo Empire). ArchDaily's "permanent" list: Sarbalé Ke, Etherea (Tresoldi, 2018), Colossal Cacti (Kovacs, 2022), The Messengers (2023), Monarchs (2024), Mutts (Stănescu, 2022). - ArchDaily, Apr 14, 2026; https://hypebeast.com/2026/4/coachella-2026-art-installations - Hypebeast (Erin Ikeuchi) - Apr 14, 2026

### Recurring pieces
- **Balloon Chain** (Robert Bose with Michael Cha, crew of 10-20): helium balloons tied at intervals along a high-strength line, lit at night. Bose stumbled on the idea at Burning Man in 2006, when he tied balloons to his bike. The longest chain was 6,000 ft, at Burning Man. At Coachella, Paris Hilton stopped by in 2015. - https://www.vice.com/en/article/the-balloon-guy-interview - VICE (Eric Sundermann) - Jun 8, 2015. LAmag (2023) calls it a "quarter-mile chain."
- **Ferris wheel:** operated by Ray Cammack Shows (Phoenix family business since 1963). The current wheel, La Grande XL, debuted in 2017, replacing the original La Grande Wheel. It arrives on 20 semis, goes up in 5 days with a 70-ton crane and a 10-person crew, has 36 air-conditioned gondolas and more than 500,000 LEDs. A ride costs $15 for 10 minutes, or $80 for a private gondola (6 people). Its palm-tree-and-roadrunner logo is used only for Coachella and Stagecoach. - https://www.recorderonline.com/features/entertainment_news/what-it-takes-to-build-coachellas-most-recognizable-icon/article_6f4e07a1-1236-5044-ad3c-81251de26b8d.html - syndicated (LA Times/TNS photo; publisher unconfirmed) - about Apr 2026
  - Conflict: Phoenix New Times gives 150 ft, the "largest transportable observation wheel," a 60-ton crane and 10 days. - https://www.phoenixnewtimes.com/arts-culture/phoenix-family-owns-and-operates-iconic-coachella-ferris-wheel-21472485/ - Phoenix New Times - undated

### What happens to the pieces
- *Escape Velocity* was to be sold. - Complex 2014
- *Spectra* was meant to come down after 2020 but stayed because of Covid delays and fan affection. NEWSUBSTANCE calls it a "permanent feature" that has appeared in Fortnite, Minecraft, Meta and Family Guy partnerships. - LAmag 2023; NEWSUBSTANCE (above)
- *Sarbalé ke* went to a park in Indio, and modular design is meant to let more works live on. - ICON 2025
- Several works stay on site as "permanent" installations. - ArchDaily 2026
- Signage is repurposed by EcoSet. - Palm Springs Life (above)

### Payment plans ("layaway")
- Introduced in 2009 as a response to sluggish sales. The 2009 options were two equal payments, or 10% down plus two installments due in March and April. About 18-19% of buyers used it. - https://www.aol.com/2009/03/06/pay-for-concert-on-the-installment-plan/ - AOL - Mar 6, 2009 (Tollett quote, 18%); TicketNews, Apr 22, 2009 (19%)
- It returned for a second year in 2010. - https://www.ticketnews.com/coachella-2010-revives-layaway-plan-for-second-year-as-ticket-presales-begin - TicketNews - 2009 (headline only, not opened)
- 2025: about 60% of attendees used a plan (per Billboard), with $49.99 down, three installments, and no interest, through AXS. A payment more than 10 days late triggers automatic cancellation and a one-year credit. - https://www.entrepreneur.com/business-news/most-coachella-attendees-buy-tickets-with-buy-now-pay-later/490596 - Entrepreneur (Sherin Shibu) - Apr 24, 2025

### Wristbands
- RFID: Intellitix had supplied RFID to Coachella for four years as of 2014, which points to about 2010-11. Pollstar headlined "Intellitix Takes RFID To Coachella" on Mar 15, 2011 (the fetch redirected and was not followed). 2014 was the first year attendees had to create individual online profiles. - https://intellitix.com/hub/coachella-festival-loves-rfid-technology-access-control - Intellitix (vendor) - about 2014
  - The brief's "RFID since 2012" is probably early. Sources point to 2011.
- 2026/27 rules: registration happens only in the Coachella app, once, with legal name, a unique email and the 6-letter code on the back. Wearing two bands blocks the scan. Damaged or tampered bands don't work. Registration helps replace lost or stolen bands (fees may apply). Activating a third-party band "doesn't guarantee" it stays valid. Passes from unauthorized sellers "can be canceled at any time." The official resale is AXS Official Resale. All sales are final, and there is an official waitlist. - https://www.coachella.com/faq - Coachella.com - accessed Oct 31, 2026
- A classic scam: the seller reports a delivered pass as "never received," gets a replacement, and resells the original, which then gets deactivated. - CV Independent, Feb 17, 2014
- "Scamchella" 2026: one buyer paid more than $2,000 for three wristbands on OfferUp. They worked on day one and were revoked on day two after the seller reported them lost or stolen. "Hundreds" lined up at will-call unsure their passes would work. Goldenvoice made no public statement. - https://www.nbcpalmsprings.com/local-and-community/2026/04/17/scamchella-surge-ticket-fraud-casts-shadow-over-festival-season - NBC Palm Springs - Apr 17, 2026
- 2018: staff were told to check wristbands meticulously, especially for VIP, and drones were used for surveillance (unnamed sources). - https://www.tmz.com/2018/04/14/coachella-crackdown-security-counterfeit-passes-wristbands/ - TMZ - Apr 14, 2018

### Sellout patterns and the 2024-25 slowdown
- 2012 was the first two-weekend year. W1 GA, VIP and camping were sold out before the general on-sale of the remaining passes. - NBC LA, Jan 13, 2012
- 2013: 158,000 tickets sold in 20 minutes (Louder's claim). - Louder, Oct 7, 2024
- Past sellout times ran from 40 minutes (2015) to just over 4 hours (2022). 2023 W1 took about 4 days. 2024 went on sale Jan 19 and was still available a week later, the "slowest in ten years" (per SFGate). 2024 W1 sold out about a month in (per Billboard). - https://www.complex.com/music/a/jaelaniturnerwilliams/coachella-ticket-sales-are-reportedly-historically-slow - Complex (Jaelani Turner-Williams) - Jan 25, 2024 (updated Feb 19, 2024)
- 2025: days after the Nov 22, 2024 presale, W1 GA was mostly gone but W2 GA was "widely available." Kendrick Lamar and Rihanna reportedly declined headline offers. - DMN, Nov 26, 2024
- 2025 was still not sold out at kickoff, and 2024 passes resold below face value. - https://sherwood.news/culture/coachella-2025-kicks-off-with-scorching-weather-and-cooling-demand - Sherwood News (Millie Giles) - Apr 11, 2025

## Best stories
- **The Do LaB snuck in.** In 2004 the Flemming brothers got the green light "a week or two" before the festival, brought speakers in after hours, and started a party in a dome. Two decades later they still book their own unannounced lineup inside Coachella, listed on the official poster only as an "art installation." (NPR 2022; Variety 2023)
- **The curfew meter.** $20,000 at 5 minutes over, then $1,000 a minute, paid into Indio's general fund. Paul McCartney paid $54K in 2009, Frank Ocean's W1 set helped run up $117K in 2023, and Bieber's 2 minutes over cost $20K in 2026. (Hypebeast, WMMO)
- **Layaway was a recession product.** It launched in 2009 against "sluggish sales," with 18-19% uptake. By 2025, about 60% of buyers used it, with $49.99 down. (AOL 2009, TicketNews 2009, Entrepreneur 2025)
- **The snail, then the astronaut.** Poetic Kinetics bolted giant creatures onto the same variable-reach forklifts that build the festival, then drove them through the crowd. The astronaut came back in 2019 looking "burnt from reentry." (PBS 2013, Complex 2014, Time Out 2019)
- **A Hollywood effects man runs the art.** Paul Clemente built miniatures for *Titanic* and *The Matrix* before becoming Coachella's art curator in 2007. His rule: the biggest, most dramatic thing that can go up in ten days. (LAmag 2023, PBS 2013)
- **1,500 tons of concrete for a tent.** The 2024 Sahara rebuild poured 156 truckloads of footings, with crews working overnight so Indio's streets stayed clear. (Desert Sun 2024)
- **Spectra wouldn't leave.** It was supposed to come down after 2020, stayed because of Covid and fan love, and ended up in Fortnite and Family Guy. (LAmag 2023; NEWSUBSTANCE)
- **A hill in five days.** For Kanye's Easter Sunday Service in 2019, crews built a sod-covered hill between weekends, with hidden speakers designed by Paul Tollett's brother. (LA Times 2019)
- **"Scamchella" is a design flaw.** Wristbands are bound to the original buyer, so a seller can resell a band, report it stolen, and kill it mid-festival. The scam was documented in 2014 and was still happening in 2026. (CV Independent 2014; NBC Palm Springs 2026)
- **The livestream is directed blind.** More than 200 crew, six trucks and one director per stage, cutting live sets with no rehearsal, on cinema cameras instead of sports gear. Its producer has done it since 2007. (Desert Sun 2024)
- **The 2026 flip back to sellouts.** After 2024's slowest sales in a decade and 2025 W2 sitting unsold, 2026 sold out in about three days. Then the 2027 advance sale's checkout reportedly crashed. (Complex, DMN, VICE, Hoodline)
