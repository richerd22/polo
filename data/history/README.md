One JSON file per Coachella year (1999-2026): the line-up by day, then by stage, acts in the page's printed order (headliner usually first).
Source: Wikipedia, "Coachella Festival line-ups" (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coachella_Festival_line-ups), page revision 1381296443, retrieved 2026-11-11.
These are transcriptions of Wikipedia, not official Goldenvoice data - cross-check against posters, coachella.com or press coverage where possible before stating anything as fact.
Weekend-only, cancelled, replacement and switched slots are moved out of act names into "notes"; acts the page marks as cancelled are left out of "acts" but listed in notes. Side stages printed as prose (Do LaB, Heineken House/Dome, Desert Sky, The Bunker) are split per weekend where the page splits them.
2000 had no festival; 2020 holds the announced line-up (status "canceled", days only, no stages); 2021 was canceled with no line-up on the page.
