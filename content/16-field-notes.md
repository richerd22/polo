short: Coachella runs the same festival twice, and Goldenvoice promised in 2011 that the two weekends would be identical - "same lineup, same art, same place, different people." They are not identical, and the gap is the most interesting thing about the second weekend. For seven editions it was the weekend nobody at home could watch. It is the cheaper one on resale. And in 2026 it was the weekend that got Madonna. These are the notes on what actually differs.
---

Goldenvoice said it plainly when it announced the second weekend on May 31, 2011: "We will attempt to produce two identical festival weekends. That means same lineup, same art, same place, different people."[^fn-tn11] The dates were April 13-15 and April 20-22, 2012, same site, same poster.[^fn-sg11] Passes were not interchangeable between the two, though you could buy into both in separate orders.[^fn-tn11]

"Attempt" turned out to be the operative word. Fifteen years on, the two weekends are the same festival in the way two pressings of the same record are the same record. This chapter is the list of differences, each one sourced. Where something is our own reading rather than a reported fact, it says so.

## The seven years weekend two was not on television

The clearest split was never on the poster. It was the broadcast.

When YouTube extended its livestream deal to cover both weekends, Fortune noted the change in one line: "In previous years the site has only streamed the first weekend of the festival."[^fn-fort19] That was January 2019, after seven held editions of the two-weekend era. The year before, a YouTube spokesperson told CNN that "none of the Coachella weekend two sets will be live streamed on YouTube"; select first-weekend clips went up on demand for 60 days, and Beyoncé's was not among them, because the performance was not cleared for it.[^fn-esq18]

Even the 2019 expansion was not parity. Weekend two got what Fortune described as a curated live experience: behind-the-scenes footage, artist vignettes and a few select performances.[^fn-fort19] Full coverage of both weekends from every stage took until 2023, when YouTube ran all six stages across both weekends for the first time, up from three.[^fn-tc23]

By 2026 the second weekend was arguably the better broadcast. YouTube's own preview for it counted seven stages, with the Yuma tent joining the digital lineup that weekend, three stages in 4K for the first time, a vertical feed from the Quasar shot on a phone, and creator watch parties running "this weekend only." Kacey Musgraves was added to the Saturday bill.[^fn-yt26]

:::numbers Weekend two on the stream
- 2012-2018: weekend one only, per Fortune's account of the deal[^fn-fort19]
- 2018: a YouTube spokesperson confirms no weekend-two sets at all[^fn-esq18]
- 2019: both weekends, but weekend two as a curated feed, not the full festival[^fn-fort19]
- 2023: all six stages, both weekends, for the first time[^fn-tc23]
- 2026: seven stages, the Yuma added for weekend two, watch parties weekend two only[^fn-yt26]
:::

That arc matters for the rest of the chapter. For most of the era, anything that happened on the second weekend happened only in front of the people standing there.

## The cheaper weekend

Demand split from the start, but gently. When 2012 passes went on sale, weekend one sold out in about 40 minutes and weekend two "within another two hours."[^fn-mxdwn12] A three-day pass was $269 plus fees, and the 2011 edition it was built to relieve had sold out in six days.[^fn-tn11]

Later the gap got wider and more public. In December 2024, Digital Music News reported weekend one still unsold and weekend two available across all tiers.[^fn-dmn24] In April 2025 it reported weekend-two passes trading below face value on StubHub and Vivid Seats.[^fn-dmn25] Even in 2018, the Esquire piece put weekend-two passes on StubHub at about $450, roughly $30 over face.[^fn-esq18]

Our reading, not a reported fact: the resale discount and the missing broadcast are the same thing seen twice. The weekend with no camera on it is the weekend with less reason to be photographed at.

## Where the identical lineup stops being identical

The poster is the same. The running order is not, and it breaks in four ways.

**Someone drops out.** Frank Ocean's 2023 withdrawal from weekend two is the biggest example: his team cited two fractures and a sprain in his left leg, and Blink-182, who had played their first reunion show with Tom DeLonge on the first Friday, took the slot.[^fn-sg23][^fn-loud23] Chapter 07 has the full account.

**Someone is rained or blown out.** Anyma's 2026 midnight set on the first Friday was called off for wind; his full *ÆDEN* show moved to weekend two.[^fn-sg26]

**The show is recast.** Sabrina Carpenter's 2026 set used a monologue performed by Susan Sarandon on the first weekend and Geena Davis, her *Thelma & Louise* co-star, on the second, in a shorter cut, with Terry Crews taking Will Ferrell's part.[^fn-wrap26]

**Two stages book the weekends separately on purpose.** The Quasar runs three-hour sets with different acts each weekend, and Do LaB announces its own bill: in 2026 Tinashe and Andy C on weekend one, Seth Troxler, A-Trak and SBTRKT on weekend two.[^fn-mic26] Neither of those is on the main poster in any readable size.

:::lore The weekend that got the guests
For most of the two-weekend era the surprise guests were a first-weekend tradition: Madonna in 2015, \*NSYNC in 2019, Shania Twain in 2022. In 2026 nearly all of the big ones landed a week later - Madonna with Sabrina Carpenter, SZA and Sexyy Red with Justin Bieber, Olivia Rodrigo with Addison Rae, Snoop Dogg with Giveon.[^fn-varietyau] Variety asked the industry why. One agent said weekend one "is driven heavily by influencer culture." An insider put it as: "Weekend two is always better... more to see and less to be seen."[^fn-varietyau] Others said the later guests protect a headliner's first-weekend spotlight. Nobody told Variety it was coordinated by Goldenvoice or YouTube.[^fn-varietyau] Treat the pattern as one strong year, not a rule - it is a single reported season, and 2027 will say whether it holds.
:::

## What to do with that

If you are choosing a weekend, the sourced differences are these: weekend two is the one more likely to discount on resale,[^fn-dmn24][^fn-dmn25] the one whose broadcast is now the fuller of the two,[^fn-yt26] the one where a cancelled set from the week before gets made up,[^fn-sg26] and, in 2026, the one the guests came to.[^fn-varietyau] Weekend one is the one that sells out first,[^fn-mxdwn12] and the one an agent on the record associates with influencer culture.[^fn-varietyau]

Goldenvoice's own promise holds on three of its four terms. Same lineup, mostly. Same art. Same place. Different people - and by now, a different festival built out of the same parts.
